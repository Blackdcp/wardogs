## Краткий ответ

**Do not chase Level 100 as a single raw grind unless the role practice itself is valuable. Pick one role loop, measure XP before and after each task, and stop using any route that depends on staged farming or an exploit fixed in 0.1.2.**

## Подтверждённые факты

- Seasonal cash and XP reset policy makes late-season grinding a decision, not an automatic recommendation.
- Update 0.1.2 tightened exploit routes, server-authoritative revive checks and low-value reward abuse.
- Press and creator coverage has turned XP pacing and endgame value into a current discussion topic.
- Role XP, Career XP and cash need to be measured separately after each task.

## План действий

### Choose one track

Assault, Medic, Support, Recon, Driver and Pilot need different loops. Mixing everything makes the result impossible to read.

### Measure one task

Record XP before and after a revive chain, transport route, supply build or objective push.

### Prefer useful loops

A route that wins fights, moves players or sustains a FOB will survive balance changes better than staged farming.

### Respect the reset clock

If Season 2 is close, practice high-skill loops rather than grinding numbers that may reset.

### Use the planner

Map the next meaningful unlock in the progression route tool before buying an expensive kit.
