There is no public WARDOGS key or WARDOGS Alpha key to claim now. The August 7-9 Closed Alpha and August 21-23 Closed Beta have both ended. No later test date is announced; the next confirmed public access is Steam Early Access on September 10, 2026.

## Key Status

No reusable code, public key list, or permanent Alpha invitation has been announced. An old code cannot reopen a completed test, and this site does not distribute keys. Search results that promise an instant Alpha unlock are using outdated language at best and attempting account theft or payment fraud at worst.

If you searched `wardogs key`, the important distinction is that a Steam entitlement is not the same thing as a transferable code. A real current access right should appear on the Steam account that requested access or bought the game; it should not require a third-party key checker, survey, attachment, or Discord direct message.

Current access should be tied to the official Steam account flow. A legitimate invitation or purchase entitlement belongs in Steam, not in a downloadable launcher, browser extension, survey reward, or direct-message attachment.

## Legacy FirstLook Route

Earlier WARDOGS testing used a FirstLook community registration flow that could involve Discord or Steam sign-in and community verification. That process explains older guides, screenshots, and questions about dashboards or account linking. It is historical context for the Alpha period, not a required step for present access.

Do not follow reconstructed button-by-button instructions from an old interface. The page state, permissions, and campaign may have changed, and the approved current access control is visible on the official Steam store page.

## Current Access

The August Closed Beta ended on August 24, 2026, so an old Playtest entitlement or Alpha key does not open an active test. No later beta or playtest date has been announced. Use the official Steam App 1867240 page for the next confirmed milestone: Windows PC Early Access on September 10, 2026. Any legitimate ownership or future test entitlement should appear directly in that Steam account.

Joining the official Discord can help you follow announcements and troubleshooting, but membership or a role does not itself guarantee access. Never interpret a community badge as a replacement for the Steam entitlement.

## Scam Safety

Reject offers that request a Steam password, Steam Guard code, Discord token, session cookie, cryptocurrency payment, gift card, or remote-control access. Do not run an executable described as a key checker or beta activator. A screenshot of a code is not proof that it is valid, unused, transferable, or relevant to the current test.

When a message claims to be from BULKHEAD, Team17, or WARDOGS, compare the announcement with the official Steam page and official Discord. Type or use a known official address instead of following a shortened sign-in link. If you already entered credentials on a suspicious page, change the password through the platform, revoke unknown sessions, and contact Steam Support.
