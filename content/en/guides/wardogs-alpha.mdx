## Quick Answer

The WARDOGS Closed Alpha is over: the documented test window was August 7-9, 2026, and the official August 11 announcement says the Alpha had run the previous weekend and lifted its NDA. New players should use the current Steam beta route, not search for an active Alpha sign-up.

## Confirmed Facts

- The documented WARDOGS Closed Alpha ran August 7-9, 2026.
- The August 11 announcement lifted the sharing restriction for that Alpha content.
- The NDA lift does not automatically apply to every private build or future test.
- Current public access attention should shift to Steam beta and Early Access routes.

## Test Timeline

The Closed Alpha was a limited, historical test of an unfinished build. Its August 7-9 window should not be confused with the Closed Beta scheduled for August 21-23 or the September Early Access release. Pages or videos that still say the Alpha is live are stale unless they clearly identify a different, newly announced test.

On August 11, WARDOGS officially allowed creators to publish footage they had banked during that Closed Alpha. The announcement keeps separate ETP playtests under NDA, so the change should not be interpreted as blanket permission for every private build. It also does not reopen access or turn Alpha observations into promises about the beta or launch build.

## What Was Tested

Public Alpha footage and official game descriptions center on the large Control Zone battle, three-team pressure, infantry and vehicles, persistent cash, team support, building, and destruction. These materials are useful for understanding the intended loop, but an Alpha is designed to expose problems and gather feedback. Individual weapons, prices, controls, construction options, performance, and interface details may already have changed.

The test also provided practical information about match readability and teamwork at scale. Treat a participant's smooth or rough performance as a report about one machine and one build, not a universal benchmark. Current system requirements and store features belong to the Steam page.

## NDA

The sharing restriction was lifted for the documented Alpha content on August 11. That means footage from authorized participants can be discussed as pre-release evidence. It does not automatically remove restrictions from every private build, private channel, survey, or future test. Creators should follow the terms attached to the build they actually received.

For readers, the safest label is "Closed Alpha footage" with its date. Avoid presenting it as current beta gameplay, a final review, or proof of features that official sources have not confirmed.

## Current Route

There is no useful reason to pursue an old Alpha invitation now. Visit the official WARDOGS Steam page, sign into the account you intend to use, and choose Request Access for a chance at the Closed Beta. The published alternative is a pre-order, which guarantees entry to the August 21-23 beta under the announced terms.

Do not pay a reseller for an "Alpha key." The completed test cannot be made current by a code, and a legitimate present-day entitlement should resolve through Steam.
