## Beta 02 schedule correction

The FPS Games Show began at **18:00 UTC on September 3**. A later official schedule notice moved the actual Beta 02 server opening to **19:00 UTC**; the closing time remains **September 6 at 08:00 UTC**. Older Steam copy and search snippets can still show 18:00 UTC as the beta start, so use 19:00 UTC for the revised server window and 18:00 UTC only for the broadcast.

The active WARDOGS Playtest event is **Closed Beta 02**, scheduled from **September 3 at 19:00 UTC to September 6 at 08:00 UTC**. Check Steam for an approved entitlement; a free request by itself is not an invitation, while the published pre-purchase route guarantees access.

## Closed Beta 02: current official status

Steam describes this as a closed scale test, not an open beta. The test covers server scaling, the browser, login queues, voice communication, Twitch Drops, and DLC entitlements. Capacity can still affect sessions. Free Request Access approvals arrive in waves; a pending request does not guarantee entry.

### What to do now

1. Open the official Steam page and sign in to the account that requested or purchased access.
2. Check the Library for the official WARDOGS entitlement and install control.
3. In the server browser, select **one region only** if the list appears empty.
4. If local voice fails, the developer suggests disabling voice chat once or opening the scoreboard and pressing **U twice**.
5. Treat Supporter Pack items as unavailable during the beta; the developer says they are intended to unlock in Early Access.
6. Record the exact error, UTC time, region, hardware, and driver before contacting support.

### After the beta

Steam Early Access remains scheduled for **September 10, 2026** on Windows PC. This guide does not invent an exact unlock hour, progress carryover, final balance, console release, or cross-play plan. Recheck Steam and official WARDOGS channels when the beta closes because a test entitlement, install state, or server list can change independently.

## How Access Works

The completed August beta used two routes with different outcomes:

1. **Request Access:** a free expression of interest that placed a Steam account in the selection pool, without guaranteed approval.
2. **Pre-order:** the published Closed Beta terms guaranteed access for the completed August 21-23 window. The purchase remains the Early Access product, but the beta benefit is historical.

Do not describe Request Access as a reservation, queue position, or guaranteed key. A visible request confirmation means Steam recorded the request; it does not mean the beta has been added to the account.

## Closed Beta

Invited accounts use the separate **WARDOGS Playtest** Steam Library entry. During Beta 02, an active install control on an entitled account is the relevant access signal; after the September 6 close, an old library entry alone will not prove that servers are active. A community role, Twitch Drop, or Discord membership never substitutes for the Steam entitlement.

## Troubleshooting

If Request Access remains pending during Beta 02, the account has not yet received a free entitlement. Qualifying pre-purchasers should verify the purchase in Steam account history and use Steam Support for transaction or ownership problems; local troubleshooting cannot grant a missing entitlement.

If the entitled test build is installed but login fails, do not repeatedly delete it or buy another copy. Check the official announcement hub and the [known issues guide](/guides/wardogs-known-issues) first. Server availability and Steam ownership are different conditions.

Ignore websites, direct messages, and downloads that claim to bypass selection. WARDOGS access should resolve through the official Steam account path. Never provide a password, Steam Guard code, session cookie, or payment outside Steam to obtain a playtest place.
