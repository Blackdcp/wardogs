The current WARDOGS playtest route is on Steam: Request Access gives you a chance to be selected, while a pre-order guarantees entry to the Closed Beta scheduled for August 21-23, 2026. No exact opening time has been announced.

## Current Status

The next published testing window is the three-day Closed Beta on August 21-23, 2026. Treat those as calendar dates, not a promise that servers open at midnight in your region. An official start time, time zone, preload window, and live server status still need confirmation from Steam or a WARDOGS announcement.

Earlier Alpha registration pages and community account flows are historical context, not the primary route for this beta. The safest current destination is Steam App 1867240. Wishlist the game if you only want updates, or use the access control that matches how you intend to join.

## How Access Works

Steam presents two routes with different outcomes:

1. **Request Access:** a free expression of interest. It places your Steam account in the selection pool, but an approval email or library entitlement is still required.
2. **Pre-order:** the published Closed Beta terms guarantee access for the August 21-23 window. Confirm the edition, regional price, and refund information shown in your own Steam store before paying.

Do not describe Request Access as a reservation, queue position, or guaranteed key. A visible request confirmation means Steam recorded the request; it does not mean the beta has been added to the account.

## Closed Beta

Before the window, check the official Steam page for an install button, a library entry, or a news post. The absence of an install control days in advance does not prove access was denied because preload has not been confirmed. Likewise, a community role or Discord membership is not a substitute for a Steam entitlement.

Progress carryover, available maps, server regions, capacity, and build changes have not been promised for this test. Plan for a pre-release build whose balance, performance, and content may change. Avoid scheduling around an unofficial countdown because no precise start time is available.

## Troubleshooting

If Request Access appears stuck, make sure you are signed into the intended Steam account, reload the store page in the Steam client, and check the email address attached to that account. If you pre-ordered, verify the purchase in Steam account history and look for the beta entitlement in the library. Use Steam Support for transaction or account problems.

Ignore websites, direct messages, and downloads that claim to bypass selection. WARDOGS access should resolve through the official Steam account path. Never provide a password, Steam Guard code, session cookie, or payment outside Steam to obtain a playtest place.
