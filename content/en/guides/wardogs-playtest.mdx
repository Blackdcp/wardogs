## September 24 status: Playtest is historical

WARDOGS launched in Steam Early Access for Windows PC on **September 10, 2026**. The main WARDOGS Steam app is the current product; the separate Playtest entry and Closed Beta 02 access rules are historical.

Use the [Early Access guide](/en/guides/wardogs-early-access) for the playable product, the [Season 2 guide](/en/guides/wardogs-season-2) for the next dated update, and the [server-status guide](/en/guides/wardogs-server-status) for interruption checks. A remaining Playtest button is not proof of an open beta.

## Quick Answer

**The WARDOGS Playtest window is closed.** Closed Beta 02 ended September 6 at 08:00 UTC. The main game launched in Steam Early Access on September 10. Use the main Steam app to play now; an old request or Playtest entitlement does not mean another test has opened.

## Confirmed Facts

The broadcast began at 18:00 UTC, one hour before the corrected **19:00 UTC** server opening, and Beta 02 ended on September 6 at 08:00 UTC. That Playtest window is historical; WARDOGS Early Access launched on September 10 through the main Steam app.

## Current Status

The old beta is not open to everyone. An old library entitlement or pending request does not establish that a test server is currently available. Community queue reports do not prove that your local installation or account is broken.

Earlier Alpha registration pages and the completed August beta flow are historical context. For Beta 02, the safest current destination is Steam App 1867240 and the account's Steam Library entitlement. After the September 6 close, wait for a newly dated official announcement before treating any remaining Playtest control as a new test window.

## How Access Works

The completed August beta used two routes with different outcomes:

1. **Request Access:** a free expression of interest that placed a Steam account in the selection pool, without guaranteed approval.
2. **Pre-order:** the published Closed Beta terms guaranteed access for the completed August 21-23 window. The purchase remains the Early Access product, but the beta benefit is historical.

Do not describe Request Access as a reservation, queue position, or guaranteed key. A visible request confirmation means Steam recorded the request; it does not mean the beta has been added to the account.

## Closed Beta

Invited accounts use the separate **WARDOGS Playtest** Steam Library entry. During Beta 02, an active install control on an entitled account is the relevant access signal; after the September 6 close, an old library entry alone will not prove that servers are active. A community role, Twitch Drop, or Discord membership never substitutes for the Steam entitlement.

## Troubleshooting

During the initial closed phase, a pending Request Access state meant the account had not received a free entitlement. Steam then opened the beta to everyone on September 5, and the whole test ended on September 6. A remaining request or library entry now proves neither current access nor a local installation fault.

If the entitled test build is installed but login fails, do not repeatedly delete it or buy another copy. Check the official announcement hub and the [known issues guide](/en/guides/wardogs-known-issues) first. Server availability and Steam ownership are different conditions.

Ignore websites, direct messages, and downloads that claim to bypass selection. WARDOGS access should resolve through the official Steam account path. Never provide a password, Steam Guard code, session cookie, or payment outside Steam to obtain a playtest place.
