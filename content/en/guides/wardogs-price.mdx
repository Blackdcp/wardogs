## Base Edition

The $39.99 figure is the United States reference for the base game, not a universal conversion target. It purchases the WARDOGS product scheduled for Windows Steam Early Access on September 10, 2026. Under the published access terms, a pre-order also guarantees entry to the August 21-23 Closed Beta.

Do not confuse the game's real-money purchase price with its in-match cash economy. The $10,000 starting balance and the cost of loadouts or vehicles are gameplay resources; they have no role in the Steam checkout price.

## Supporter Edition

The verified US reference for the Supporter Edition is $49.99. Review the current Steam edition comparison before buying so you understand exactly which extras are listed. The name alone should not be used to invent weapons, early access privileges, upgrade paths, or exclusive gameplay advantages.

Both edition listings can change before release. The store page and cart are authoritative for contents, availability, and the amount charged to the signed-in account.

## Regions

Steam applies regional price tables rather than converting the US amount at a live exchange rate. Currency, purchasing-power adjustments, local taxes, payment fees, and publisher updates can produce a different number. A converted $39.99 figure therefore is not a reliable price for Europe, the United Kingdom, Japan, Brazil, or any other market.

Sign into Steam, set the correct store country under Steam's rules, and read the local product page. Do not use a VPN, false address, or cross-region payment method to chase another market's price; that can conflict with platform terms and may complicate support.

## Refund Context

WARDOGS is an Early Access purchase, so buyers should evaluate the current build, not only the roadmap. Before checkout, read Steam's displayed refund terms, the edition contents, system requirements, release status, and any beta-access language. Refund eligibility is controlled by Steam and the transaction record, not by this guide.

The developer says the fuller release is planned to be priced higher than Early Access, but no final price or effective date is confirmed. Discounts and price changes are also possible. Always use the amount in your own cart immediately before payment.
