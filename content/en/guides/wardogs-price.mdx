## Base Edition

The $39.99 figure is the United States reference for the base game, not a universal conversion target. It purchases the WARDOGS product scheduled for Windows Steam Early Access on September 10, 2026. The old pre-order promise of August 21-23 Closed Beta entry has expired because that test ended on August 24; it is not a current access benefit.

Do not confuse the game's real-money purchase price with its in-match cash economy. The $10,000 starting balance and the cost of loadouts or vehicles are gameplay resources; they have no role in the Steam checkout price.

## Supporter Edition

The verified US reference for the Supporter Edition is $49.99. Review the current Steam edition comparison before buying so you understand exactly which extras are listed. The name alone should not be used to invent weapons, early access privileges, upgrade paths, or exclusive gameplay advantages.

Both edition listings can change before release. The store page and cart are authoritative for contents, availability, and the amount charged to the signed-in account.

The August 11 official edition breakdown lists three weapon camos, **Valkyra A-91**, **Lonestar Bushmaster**, and **Manticore KH-2002**, plus the Littlebird **Taxi** camo, a hideable supporter scoreboard icon, and grenade and helmet bobbleheads. The verified US reference prices remain **$39.99** for the base edition and **$49.99** for the Supporter Edition; check your regional Steam cart before paying.

## Price Ladder

BULKHEAD's August developer video frames the Early Access price as part of a staged plan rather than a simple launch discount. ## Regions

Steam applies regional price tables rather than converting the US amount at a live exchange rate. Currency, purchasing-power adjustments, local taxes, payment fees, and publisher updates can produce a different number. A converted $39.99 figure therefore is not a reliable price for Europe, the United Kingdom, Japan, Brazil, or any other market.

Sign into Steam, set the correct store country under Steam's rules, and read the local product page. Do not use a VPN, false address, or cross-region payment method to chase another market's price; that can conflict with platform terms and may complicate support.

## Is It Worth the Current Price?

Evaluate the build that can be played, not only the version described for one or two years later. WARDOGS already pitches a distinctive combination of 100-player, three-team Control Zone warfare, persistent cash, flexible support roles, vehicles, construction, destruction, and proximity communication. That combination may justify the Early Access price for a player who enjoys coordinated sandbox battles and accepts balance or performance changes.

Wait if your purchase depends on a confirmed console version, a fixed competitive balance, a finished metagame, a specific future vehicle, or release-level stability. Current “worth it” videos can show real match texture and performance on one machine, but they remain individual experiences from a particular beta build. They cannot promise your region's server experience, hardware performance, or future content cadence.

Before buying, check the live Steam price, current user-access status, system requirements, recent developer notices, and whether friends will play in the same region and window. A cheaper Early Access entry is only useful when the current product already offers enough value for you.
