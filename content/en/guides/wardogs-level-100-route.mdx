## Quick Answer

Plan toward the next useful role unlock before chasing Level 100. Measure one repeatable team task at a time and separate role XP from Career XP. No verified universal fastest route applies to every server and patch.

## Confirmed Facts

Assault, Medic, Support, Recon, Driver and Pilot are distinct tracks. Update 0.1.2 addresses exploit routes and revive checks. Seasonal cash and XP reset under the published policy; a late-season grind is useful practice, not a promised Season 2 head start.

## Action Plan

1. Choose the target item and read its exact role/level requirement.
2. Record current role XP and Career XP separately.
3. Time a useful task such as reviving, transporting, supplying or contesting a tower.
4. Include travel, deaths and queue time when comparing the result.
5. Stop at a useful unlock or practice goal and recheck after balance changes.

## Worked example

If a target requires Driver progression, a rising Career level alone does not satisfy it. Use the progression route tool to identify the track, then compare several normal transport runs. A creator reaching 100 shows that run, not the time your account will need.

## Common mistake

Do not extrapolate one unusually profitable action into an hours-to-100 promise. Staged farming and already-patched exploits are not a durable route. Preserve cash for playable kits and record what you learn before the seasonal transition.
