## Quick Answer

**WARDOGS Season 2 launches on October 15, 2026.** BULKHEAD published the date in the official Season 02 teaser on September 22. The teaser uses rain, lightning, and dark storm conditions as its clearest visual signal, but it does not publish a feature list, patch number, exact launch hour, new map, weapon list, balance table, or a Season 02 transition schedule.

The **general reset policy is not a rumor**: BULKHEAD's [April 7 Early Access & Beyond video at 03:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PQvtvAvl-78&t=185s) says that at the end of a season cash and XP progression reset, remaining cash automatically converts into Gold Bars, and Gold Bars and cosmetics persist across seasons. October 15 is the Season 02 start date, but the teaser does not say exactly when the Season 1 reset runs or at what exchange rate. Treat the official Steam announcement as the calendar source and wait for a transition notice before making rate-sensitive Gold Bar decisions.

## Confirmed Facts

- Official name: **WARDOGS Season 02**.
- Official release date: **October 15, 2026**.
- Announcement published: **September 22, 2026** on Steam.
- The official teaser visibly presents wet, stormy battlefield conditions.
- The announcement says to stay tuned for more details; it does not contain patch notes.
- An earlier official video states the seasonal cash/XP reset and Gold Bar carryover policy; it is separate from the short Season 02 teaser.

The weather imagery is strong evidence of the season's theme, not proof of a complete dynamic-weather system. The teaser does not explain whether rain is global, map-specific, cosmetic, randomized, or mechanically linked to visibility, traction, aircraft, sound, or performance.

## What Is Not Confirmed Yet

No first-party Season 2 note currently names new weapons, vehicles, maps, factions, modes, unlock levels, prices, XP curves, server-browser changes, or bug fixes. It also does not state whether Season 1 ends at a particular hour or whether Season 2 starts after maintenance.

The teaser does not supply the **operational transition details**. The earlier official video already states that cash and XP progression reset, while Gold Bars and cosmetics persist. It does not give a Season 02 reset hour, exchange rate at the boundary, complete item-by-item treatment of purchased unlocks or settings, or proof that planned permanent Player Skills are live. Check those against BULKHEAD's transition notice rather than replacing a confirmed policy with speculation.

## 12-Day End-of-Season Capital Strategy

With only 12 days remaining before the October 15 reset, apply these three rules to maximize your account's enduring value:

1. **Zero Out Unused Cash into Gold**: Liquid cash will be swept at the seasonal boundary. Avoid hoarding high cash sums without a plan; visit the HQ Quartermaster to exchange spare balances into permanent Gold Bars.
2. **Deploy High-Tier Unlocks While They Are Live**: If you have earned high-tier assets like the Heavy Tank or Artillery Tank, deploy and enjoy them in Season 1 combat. There is no benefit to saving cash reserves for post-wipe play.
3. **Archive Sensitivity and Keybind Configurations**: Major seasonal client updates often introduce engine rewrites or config changes. Save your sensitivity, keybinds, and graphics settings in a separate document before patch day.

## FAQ

**Is October 15 only an estimate?** No. It is printed in the official Steam announcement. The launch hour remains unannounced.

**Should I stop leveling because of a wipe?** Seasonal cash and XP reset are the developer's stated plan. Whether to keep leveling for Season 1 play is your choice; do not treat earned XP as a promised Season 2 head start. The exact reset hour still needs a transition notice.
