## Quick Answer

**WARDOGS Season 2 launches on October 15, 2026.** BULKHEAD published the date in the official Season 02 teaser on September 23. The teaser uses rain, lightning, and dark storm conditions as its clearest visual signal, but it does not publish a feature list, patch number, exact launch hour, new map, weapon list, balance table, or progression-reset policy.

That distinction matters. October 15 is confirmed; most lists of Season 2 weapons, vehicles, maps, and wipe rules are still predictions. Treat the official Steam announcement as the calendar source and wait for a full changelog before changing a long-term progression or spending plan.

## Confirmed Facts

- Official name: **WARDOGS Season 02**.
- Official release date: **October 15, 2026**.
- Announcement published: **September 23, 2026** on Steam.
- The official teaser visibly presents wet, stormy battlefield conditions.
- The announcement says to stay tuned for more details; it does not contain patch notes.

The weather imagery is strong evidence of the season's theme, not proof of a complete dynamic-weather system. The teaser does not explain whether rain is global, map-specific, cosmetic, randomized, or mechanically linked to visibility, traction, aircraft, sound, or performance.

## What Is Not Confirmed Yet

No first-party Season 2 note currently names new weapons, vehicles, maps, factions, modes, unlock levels, prices, XP curves, server-browser changes, or bug fixes. It also does not state whether Season 1 ends at a particular hour or whether Season 2 starts after maintenance.

The teaser does not answer the most important progression question: what, if anything, resets. Do not convert an interview estimate, an in-game interpretation, a creator title, or another game's seasonal model into a guaranteed WARDOGS wipe. Cash, role XP, Career level, unlocks, statistics, settings, and any long-term currency must be checked separately when BULKHEAD publishes the transition rules.

## What Players Search For

The highest-intent questions are the Season 2 date, the possibility of rain or weather, a progression wipe, new guns, a new map, and whether current unlock grinding is still worthwhile. Only the date is calendar-confirmed today. The storm presentation is visible in official media; everything else needs a later official source or live-build verification.

## How to Use This Guide

Use this page as a confirmation ledger, not a rumor board. Read the date first, then compare any new claim with the official Steam feed. A real patch claim should name the affected system and provide either a first-party changelog or a dated live-build observation. If a source only says “Season 2 will be big,” it does not establish a specific feature.

## Preparation Checklist

1. Finish time-sensitive Season 1 goals without assuming their progress will transfer.
2. Keep a record of current cash, Career level, role levels, unlocks, settings, and keybinds.
3. Avoid buying around an unconfirmed balance leak or creator tier list.
4. Leave storage headroom for a client update; the official download size is not announced.
5. Recheck the official feed for maintenance time, patch notes, and reset scope during launch week.
6. After updating, verify vendor prices, progression tracks, graphics settings, controls, and server filters before using an old guide.

## FAQ

**Is October 15 only an estimate?** No. It is printed in the official Steam announcement. The launch hour remains unannounced.

**Does the teaser confirm dynamic weather?** It confirms the storm presentation shown in official media. It does not define the system's scope or gameplay effects.

**Should I stop leveling because of a wipe?** Not on the teaser alone. Continue only with the risk level you accept, and wait for an explicit first-party transition notice before treating any reset as certain.
