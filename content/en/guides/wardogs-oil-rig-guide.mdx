## Quick Answer

**Is the Oil Rig / Drill Rig confirmed for Season 1?** The [official September 9 Season 1 changelog](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1867240/view/701027323413004455) does not describe the Oil Rig or Drill Rig. The checked first-party source provides no current construction steps, placement rule, fuel requirement, activation timing, or Hot Zone outcome for it. We therefore cannot give a verified Season 1 build sequence or confirm that the beta rig is available unchanged.

**What can you use here?** The August **Closed Beta** community discussion and creator footage describe building a rig at a FOB, supplying fuel, and activating it to pull the Hot Zone. That Oil Rig / Drill Rig workflow is historical beta evidence, not verified for Season 1. Check the live build menu and map before committing resources. The separately confirmed Season 1 Large Hammer unlock and prices below do not validate the rig workflow.

## Oil Rig and Drill Rig Naming

Closed Beta community explanations and creator footage use both names for the same described battlefield job: a player-built structure that influences the Hot Zone. The checked official Season 1 changelog does not confirm either name or the structure's current availability. Use the live interface to identify what, if anything, can be built now.

In the beta accounts, the rig was distinct from map towers: a tower was an existing objective with a terminal and code process, while the rig was player-built and used FOB logistics. That distinction is a historical account, not a Season 1 mechanics guarantee.

## Closed Beta: Establish the FOB First

In the recorded beta workflow, players chose a location that could influence a useful route while remaining reachable by supply vehicles. The FOB needed valid placement, construction room, an unloading path, and enough cover for the core and rig. A position directly on the hottest ridge could reveal the project before builders finished it.

The beta planning advice was to build the smallest functional base first, protect the FOB core, leave a controlled entrance for trucks, and establish observation. This remains a way to evaluate old footage, not a verified current Oil Rig recipe.

## Closed Beta: Fuel the Rig

In the beta account, construction did not automatically make the asset operational. The reported process required **fuel supplies** stored in or available to the FOB. Beta logistics teams asked for fuel specifically rather than a generic “supplies” request; a truck full of ammunition did not satisfy that observed requirement.

The [cargo guide](/en/guides/wardogs-cargo-guide) explains supply transfer. In the beta workflow, teams kept the unloading area clear and checked that the pallet entered the FOB's usable resource pool rather than sitting nearby. Verify the present UI before treating that as a current rig activation fix.

## Closed Beta: Activate and Read the Map

After construction and fuel in the beta footage, the rig used an activation state or cooldown. Teams planned to defend the resulting Hot Zone movement; drawing attention to an undefended FOB risked losing the investment. The activation state and map effect have not been reverified for Season 1.

The beta defense plan included infantry on likely approaches, a medic, threat-specific anti-vehicle or anti-air coverage, and a logistics route. Players watched the map after activation and called out movement. The exact speed, radius, priority, and interaction with another team's tower or rig were not officially documented in the checked sources.

## Closed Beta: Rig vs Tower Code

**Tower workflow in beta accounts:** capture the existing tower terminal, collect or read code information, use the keypad, and attempt to pull the Hot Zone toward a tower.

**Rig workflow in beta accounts:** establish a FOB, build the Oil or Drill Rig, supply it with fuel, wait for its activation state, and pull the Hot Zone toward the player-built position.

The collected beta sources do not establish a permanent priority rule between these routes. Observe the current UI rather than assuming either beta interaction works the same way now.

## Closed Beta: Defend, Resupply, or Abandon

The beta strategy protected the FOB core, rig, fuel, builders, and access road as one system. Cover and controlled entrances helped without trapping friendly vehicles. This tactical advice is conditional on the structure and its logistics still functioning as observed.

When the Control Zone or match state moved beyond the position's value, teams weighed another activation against fuel and defenders. The current game's actual costs and map feedback should govern any comparable decision.
