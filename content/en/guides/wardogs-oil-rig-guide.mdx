## Quick Answer

In the Closed Beta, players built an **Oil Rig**, also called a **Drill Rig**, inside a valid FOB area with the large hammer tier. The structure consumed build resources to construct and required fuel before activation. After the cooldown, activating it pulled the **Hot Zone** toward that FOB. Every numeric cost, cooldown, placement limit, and Hot Zone interaction remains build-sensitive.

## Oil Rig and Drill Rig Naming

Community explanations and creator footage use both names for the same described battlefield job: a player-built structure that influences the Hot Zone. The Early Access interface is authoritative if the final English name changes. Search pages should mention both terms so a player can find the mechanic without presenting two separate structures.

Do not confuse the rig with map towers. A tower is an existing objective with a terminal and code process. The rig is built by players, belongs to a FOB logistics network, consumes supplies, and needs protection.

## Establish the FOB First

Choose a location that can influence a useful route while remaining reachable by supply vehicles. The FOB must have valid placement, construction room, an unloading path, and enough cover for the core and rig. A dramatic position directly on the hottest ridge may reveal the project before builders finish it.

Build the smallest functional base first. Protect the FOB core, leave a controlled entrance for trucks, and establish observation. The Oil Rig is not a substitute for walls, defenders, supply, or a route back to the objective.

## Hammer and Build Supplies

Beta building footage placed the rig behind the **large hammer** or highest demonstrated construction tier. Carry the correct hammer and enough build supplies before committing to the site. If the option is missing, check tool tier, progression, available resources, FOB radius, terrain collision, and the current build menu.

Exact beta resource values should not be treated as permanent. The useful workflow is stable: correct FOB, correct tool, valid preview, sufficient construction resource, and enough time for builders to finish without exposing every defender.

## Fuel the Rig

Construction does not automatically make the asset operational. The beta process required **fuel supplies** stored in or available to the FOB. Ask logistics for fuel specifically rather than a generic “supplies” request. A truck full of ammunition cannot activate a fuel-dependent structure.

Use the [cargo guide](/guides/wardogs-cargo-guide) to choose a vehicle and complete the transfer. Keep the unloading area clear and protect the final approach. If the interface shows no fuel after delivery, confirm the pallet was transferred into the FOB's usable resource pool rather than dropped nearby.

## Activate and Read the Map

After construction and fuel, the rig used an activation state or cooldown. Before activating, confirm the squad can exploit and defend the resulting Hot Zone movement. Pulling value toward an empty FOB only tells enemies where the expensive asset is located.

Assign infantry to the likely approaches, a medic to the holding group, anti-vehicle or anti-air coverage according to the threat, and a logistics route for repeated use. Watch the map after activation and communicate the new movement. The exact speed, radius, priority, and interaction with another team's tower or rig were not officially documented.

## Rig vs Tower Code

**Tower workflow:** capture the existing tower terminal, collect or read code information, use the keypad, and attempt to pull the Hot Zone toward a tower.

**Rig workflow:** establish a FOB, build the Oil or Drill Rig, supply it with fuel, wait for its activation state, and pull the Hot Zone toward the player-built position.

These routes may compete or interact in the live match, but the collected beta sources do not establish a permanent priority rule. Observe the current UI instead of promising that the newest activation always wins.

## Defend, Resupply, or Abandon

Protect the FOB core, rig, fuel, builders, and access road as one system. A wall without observation can be bypassed; a rig without fuel becomes decoration; a truck route without escort becomes the enemy's income opportunity. Use cover and controlled entrances without trapping friendly vehicles.

When the Control Zone or match state moves beyond the position's value, decide whether another activation is worth the fuel and defenders. Do not lose multiple squads protecting an impressive structure that no longer helps scoring. Preserve mobile assets and rebuild only when the strategic return is clear.

## Common Problems

**Rig missing from menu:** verify the large hammer, progression, FOB area, resources, and live build availability.

**Cannot place it:** move away from collisions, uneven terrain, blocked structures, and the FOB edge; read the placement preview.

**Built but cannot activate:** check the fuel pool, cooldown or activation state, and whether another mechanic is currently limiting it.

**Hot Zone did not move as expected:** inspect tower or competing rig activity and treat the current map feedback as authoritative.

## Confirmed Facts

- WARDOGS officially includes persistent cash, construction, vehicles, and logistics.
- Beta discussion connects the Oil Rig or Drill Rig workflow with a valid Hot Zone and supplied resources.
- Fuel is part of the observed logistics cycle.
- Price, fuel rate, cycle time, income, and structure limit are not confirmed for Early Access.

Players usually need to know why the Oil Rig will not place, where Hot Zone matters, what fuel to deliver, and whether the operation is profitable. The answer requires checking location, construction, supply, defense, and total risk together.

## How to Use This Guide

Use the placement sequence before committing a truck, the defense section while the rig is active, and the profitability test before rebuilding a lost operation. Refer to the cargo guide for current pallet controls.

## FAQ

**Is Oil Rig the same as Drill Rig?** Both names appear around the observed drilling structure; verify the current interface label.

**Why is placement invalid?** Check the Hot Zone, terrain, obstruction, radius, ownership, and available resources.

**Are beta profits final?** No. Measure current cost, fuel, cycle time, and payout in the live build.
