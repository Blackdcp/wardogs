## Are PS5 and Xbox versions confirmed?

The official WARDOGS launch trailer announces **console versions for 2028**. That replaces the older “PC only with no console plan” status. However, it does not name PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Switch 2, or another specific console, and it gives no day or month.

WARDOGS is currently playable in Steam Early Access on Windows PC. A 2028 console window does not confirm cross-play, cross-progression, controller features, editions, pricing, beta access, or whether all consoles launch together.

## What players should watch next

A platform-specific store page, publisher release, age rating, or official FAQ would confirm the exact targets. The accurate answer is: **consoles are announced for 2028; PS5 and Xbox are plausible but not individually confirmed by name**.

## Quick Answer

Console versions are officially planned for 2028, but PS5 and Xbox are not named individually and exact dates, cross-play, and cross-progression remain unconfirmed.

## Confirmed Facts

- WARDOGS is announced for Windows PC through Steam.
- Steam Early Access launched on September 10, 2026.
- No PS5 store page, release date, console beta, cross-play plan, or cross-progression promise has been announced.
- Controller support on PC would not by itself confirm a PlayStation version.

## Controller Support

Controller support is a separate question from PS5. A PC game can detect a gamepad without having a console version, and a console version can be planned later without proving cross-play or shared progression. Steam's current WARDOGS listing should be treated as the authority for official input labels, controller tags, and Windows requirements.

If you need a controller-first experience, wait for the current Steam page, Team17, or BULKHEAD to state the supported input model clearly. Alpha reports or creator footage may show gamepad behavior on PC, but that does not confirm PlayStation certification, DualSense features, aim assist tuning, rebinding, or launch-day console support.
