## Are PS5 and Xbox versions confirmed?

The official WARDOGS launch trailer announces **console versions for 2028** without naming systems. Team17's [Press and Creator Hub](https://www.team17.com/press-and-creator-hub) lists **PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S** as platforms. These are planned console targets, not versions available to play today. The 2028 window is not a platform-specific release date.

WARDOGS is currently playable in Steam Early Access on Windows PC. A 2028 console window does not confirm cross-play, cross-progression, controller features, editions, pricing, beta access, or whether all consoles launch together.

## What players should watch next

The platforms are already named by the publisher. Watch for platform-specific store pages or announcements that establish dates, editions, pricing, and online features. The accurate answer is: **PS5 and Xbox Series X|S are planned; a release date for either platform has not been announced**.

## Quick Answer

Team17 names PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S as planned platforms, while the launch trailer gives a broad 2028 console window. Neither version is available yet, and platform-specific dates, cross-play, and cross-progression are unconfirmed.
