## Quick Answer

Keep enough cash for playable kits, spend on practice you will use, and compare any Gold conversion with the current exchange screen. Cash/XP reset and Gold/cosmetic retention are announced policy; the exact final rate and hour remain unannounced.

## Confirmed Facts

Season 2 is announced for October 15. Developers say remaining cash converts to Gold at season end. In the October interview, existing market products are described as continuing across seasons except the black-and-gold AK rotation example; an item leaving sale does not remove one you already own.

## A spending decision for each remaining session

Before October 15, list the sessions you realistically expect to play, one functional kit at the current price, unpaid unlock fees and any vehicle you genuinely need. Reserve cash for those sessions before pricing a cosmetic. An unlock only earns practice value if you will actually use it; no refund or full unlock retention is assumed.

| Your position | Practical choice | Recheck before confirming |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You will keep playing and cash is tight | Keep playable equipment funded; postpone a cosmetic purchase | Next kit, unpaid unlock and transport cost |
| You have surplus cash and a specific cosmetic in mind | Compare the live offer for only the missing Gold | Target price, owned Gold and remaining cash after the exchange |
| You have no purchase target or cannot play again | Avoid a rushed purchase made only because a reset is approaching | Official automatic-conversion policy; final rate and hour remain unknown |
| You fear the shop rotation | Check the named item's current listing | Leaving sale and deleting an owned item are different claims |

**Hypothetical quantity example, not a market price:** if the desired cosmetic costs 120 Gold and you already own 95, the shortfall is 25 Gold. Obtain the current offer for that amount, if the exchange supports it, then compare the resulting cash spend with the session reserve. If the offer uses another quantity, use the displayed totals. Do not assume a linear rate, a reverse exchange or a guaranteed better rate on wipe day.

Keep screenshots of the quote and completed purchase, with date/timezone and build. If the resulting balance differs, stop repeating the transaction and send one clear support report. Player complaints in the [economy feedback forum](https://discord.com/channels/1464219389913071646/1551954554579451924/threads/1556534170799448075) identify this decision pressure; they do not establish today's rate or cancel the stated Gold/cosmetic retention policy.

## Action Plan

1. Write down cash, Gold, cosmetics, Career XP and each role level separately.
2. Reserve enough cash for the kits and practice sessions you intend to play.
3. Compare a cosmetic’s current Gold cost and your live exchange quote.
4. Buy only when you value the item or the practice, not because of an unverified wipe rumor.
5. Keep purchase records and check the official transition notice.

## Worked example

If you have enough Gold for a cosmetic but would need all your cash for another one, compare that second purchase with several playable sessions. Saving Gold is explicitly discussed by developers; there is no need to spend every bar merely because the season changes.

## Common mistake

Do not treat the live quote as the season-end rate or assume that a one-time unlock fee includes every future purchase. Record unknown unlock-retention rules instead of promising that all purchases survive.
