## Quick Answer

**Protect playable cash first, convert only surplus after checking the live exchange screen, and spend the final days on unlock knowledge, role practice and reusable route memory rather than hoarding a huge unplayed balance.**

This page is written as a current-build traffic capture page: it gives one direct answer, points to the relevant tool, and keeps uncertain values labelled until a primary source confirms them.

## Confirmed Facts

- BULKHEAD has confirmed the October 15 Season 02 date.
- The general seasonal policy says cash and XP reset while remaining cash converts to Gold Bars.
- Gold Bars and cosmetics are the confirmed persistent value bucket; the exact final conversion rate remains unannounced.
- Update 0.1.2 changed cash and XP exploit routes, so old farming advice needs a current-build check.

## Action Plan

### Keep a combat reserve

Do not spend below the amount needed for two practical kits. A dead account cannot learn routes or verify new values before reset.

### Convert only surplus

Use the live Gold exchange screen for spare cash after the reserve. Do not treat older creator rates as a promise for the Season 02 boundary.

### Buy practice, not trophies

If a vehicle or specialist tool teaches a Season 2-relevant skill, buying it for practice can beat holding cash that will reset.

### Record unlock gates

Write down role, career and vendor gates before the wipe. Knowledge persists even if the account values reset.

### Avoid exploit income

The 0.1.2 notes tightened cash and XP abuse routes. Repeated farming can cost more than it earns.
