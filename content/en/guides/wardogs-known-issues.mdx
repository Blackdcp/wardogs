## September 15 known-issues desk

WARDOGS is live in Early Access on **Patch 0.11**. The maintenance window ran on September 14 at 08:00 UTC and shipped the rebuilt server browser, cash-exploit fixes, more GPU crash coverage, and extra Asia capacity.

That does not mean every crash, queue, kick, voice fault, or performance problem is resolved. Hardware-specific reports remain community observations unless the affected build and cause are confirmed. Record region, build, CPU, GPU, driver, RAM, error text, and UTC time.

Two launch-period problems have been officially addressed and should no longer be treated as open: the login queue failure fixed by Launch Stability Hotfix #1, and the wave of **erroneous launch bans** caused by an account-link bug, which BULKHEAD said it is reversing centrally with no action needed from players.

The most discussed unresolved behavior is **mid-match team switching**. Players report teammates switching sides to dismantle FOB defenses from inside, scout, or teamkill. BULKHEAD has confirmed a design fix is being decided; team-switching controls are listed as future work, not as a shipped Patch 0.11 feature.

Do not disable anti-cheat, install unknown network tools, or follow exploit tutorials. The official policy says repeated economy abuse and XP farming can result in resets or bans.

## Quick Answer

WARDOGS is live in Steam Early Access on Patch 0.11. The September 14 maintenance has completed, launch queue failures and erroneous bans have been addressed, and team switching remains an open issue pending a design fix.

## Confirmed Facts

These workarounds came from the official Beta 02 known-issues thread. They are build-specific and must be retested in Early Access. Server or third-party voice outages cannot always be fixed on the player's PC.

## What Players Search For

The highest-value checks are server list, login access, crashes, FPS and memory behavior, voice chat, and missing Supporter Pack items. Each section below separates an official workaround from local diagnostic steps.

## Historical Beta Window and Access

Closed Beta 02 ran from **September 3 at 19:00 UTC through September 6 at 08:00 UTC** and has ended. Paid Steam pre-purchases received guaranteed beta access, while free Playtest requests were admitted in waves. Those were test-period rules; a remaining entitlement does not prove that servers are still available.

Use the Steam Library entry delivered to your account. Do not download replacement launchers, bypass anti-cheat, share Steam Guard codes, or use unsupported platform commands to force entry.

## Empty Server Browser

The developer's known-issues post tells players to select **one server region only**. If more than one region is selected, no servers may appear. Apply that first, refresh once, and then check official status channels before reinstalling anything.

An empty list can also reflect temporary maintenance, capacity, or a session problem. After Patch 0.11, also check that you are on the expected **Official** or **Community** tab and use **Show Empty/Full**, because full servers can be hidden by default.

## Crashes and Anti-Cheat

For a repeatable crash, restart Windows and Steam, verify WARDOGS files, remove unofficial launch options, update the GPU driver from its vendor, and temporarily close capture or overlay tools. Keep Windows security and anti-cheat enabled. Never delete security updates or run an unknown repair executable from chat.

Patch 0.11 addressed additional GPU crash paths, so update the game and your GPU driver before applying deeper tweaks. Keep Windows security and anti-cheat enabled. Never delete security updates or run an unknown repair executable from chat.

If you were banned during the launch window without cause, do not create a second account. BULKHEAD attributed a wave of bans to an account-link bug and said they are being reversed centrally.

## Performance and Memory

Players are reporting uneven frame rates, high RAM or VRAM use, and different results from DLSS, DLAA, shadows, mesh quality, and NVIDIA Reflex + Boost. These are **community observations tied to specific hardware and the Beta 02 build**, not confirmed universal fixes.

Start from the in-game preset, monitor actual frame time and memory use, lower one expensive setting at a time, and test the same location. Do not copy a creator's final numbers without matching resolution, GPU, CPU, driver, and build.

## Voice Chat and Audio

For lost local voice chat, the developer suggested turning voice chat off in settings or opening the scoreboard and pressing **U twice** to toggle mute and unmute. The same notice says outages at the third-party voice provider may occur. Recheck the selected Windows input/output devices before assuming a permanent account fault.

## Supporter Pack and Progress

The official beta notice said Supporter Pack items were not available during Beta 02. The pack is now sold as Steam DLC, so a current missing-item report should be checked against the Steam DLC entitlement, purchase history, and live build rather than the old Playtest client.

No page on this wiki promises that Beta 02 cash, XP, unlocks, or statistics will carry into Early Access. Treat test progression as temporary until an official statement says otherwise.

## Mid-Match Team Switching

The most reported unresolved behavior of launch week is players switching between the three teams during a match. Reported uses include dismantling a friendly FOB from inside, scouting for the enemy, and teamkilling at spawn. Matches can last an hour, so one switch can undo a long build.

BULKHEAD has publicly acknowledged the problem and says a design fix is being decided. Team-switching controls are also listed as future work in the Patch 0.11 notes, which means the current behavior is known and unpatched, not intended.

Until it changes, treat FOB access as untrusted: close doors and gates, keep critical structures behind an airlock, watch for teammates loitering at defenses, and record the player name, UTC time, and match ID when reporting. Do not install third-party "spy detection" tools; they offer no protection and can violate anti-cheat.

## Launch Queues, DDoS, and What Was Fixed

Day-one access failed because too many players requested a Steam login ticket at once and the queue could not admit them fast enough; tickets then expired before the player reached the front. BULKHEAD has also said repeated DDoS attacks hit the opening day.

Launch Stability Hotfix #1 changed the flow so a fresh ticket is issued at the front of the queue instead of at client start, and players were then admitted in waves while the team monitored results. If a queue returns, stay in it: restarting sends you to the back and makes the problem harder to diagnose.

## Erroneous Bans

A wave of launch bans was issued in error, including legitimate alpha testers and high-profile streamers. BULKHEAD confirmed the cause as an account-link and manual-review problem and said the bans are being reversed centrally, with no action required from players. Do not create a second account and do not reinstall; either action can complicate the reversal.

## Escalation Checklist
