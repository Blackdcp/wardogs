## Quick Answer

For an empty server browser, select **one region only**. For local voice failure, disable voice once or open the scoreboard and press **U twice**. Supporter Pack content is unavailable in the beta and is intended for Early Access. No single universal crash fix is confirmed.

## Confirmed Facts

These workarounds come from the official known-issues thread. They are build-specific and may change during Closed Beta 02. Server or third-party voice outages cannot always be fixed on the player's PC.

## What Players Search For

The highest-value checks are server list, login access, crashes, FPS and memory behavior, voice chat, and missing Supporter Pack items. Each section below separates an official workaround from local diagnostic steps.

## How to Use This Guide

Start with the exact symptom, apply one change at a time, record the result and UTC time, then escalate with hardware, driver, region, and error details.


WARDOGS Closed Beta 02 is a live scale test, so a queue, an empty server browser, a crash, or voice failure can be a service or build problem rather than a fault with your PC. Start with the official checks below, change one thing at a time, and preserve the exact error code before escalating.

## Current Window and Access

Closed Beta 02 is listed from **September 3 at 19:00 UTC through September 6 at 08:00 UTC**. Paid Steam pre-purchases receive guaranteed access, while free Playtest requests are admitted in waves. A pending request is not an entitlement, and an entitlement does not guarantee that every server has room.

Use the Steam Library entry delivered to your account. Do not download replacement launchers, bypass anti-cheat, share Steam Guard codes, or use unsupported platform commands to force entry.

## Empty Server Browser

The developer's known-issues post tells players to select **one server region only**. If more than one region is selected, no servers may appear. Apply that first, refresh once, and then check official status channels before reinstalling anything.

An empty list can also reflect temporary maintenance, capacity, or a session problem. Screenshots from another region do not prove that your account is broken. Record the selected region, local time, Steam entitlement state, and any message shown by the client.

## Crashes and Anti-Cheat

For a repeatable crash, restart Windows and Steam, verify WARDOGS files, remove unofficial launch options, update the GPU driver from its vendor, and temporarily close capture or overlay tools. Keep Windows security and anti-cheat enabled. Never delete security updates or run an unknown repair executable from chat.

If the same failure returns, save the visible reference code, crash time, CPU, GPU, RAM, driver version, and the last action before the crash. That evidence is more useful to support than repeatedly changing unrelated settings.

## Performance and Memory

Players are reporting uneven frame rates, high RAM or VRAM use, and different results from DLSS, DLAA, shadows, mesh quality, and NVIDIA Reflex + Boost. These are **community observations tied to specific hardware and the current beta build**, not confirmed universal fixes.

Start from the in-game preset, monitor actual frame time and memory use, lower one expensive setting at a time, and test the same location. Do not copy a creator's final numbers without matching resolution, GPU, CPU, driver, and build.

## Voice Chat and Audio

For lost local voice chat, the developer suggested turning voice chat off in settings or opening the scoreboard and pressing **U twice** to toggle mute and unmute. The same notice says outages at the third-party voice provider may occur. Recheck the selected Windows input/output devices before assuming a permanent account fault.

## Supporter Pack and Progress

The official beta notice says Supporter Pack items are not available during the beta and should unlock at Early Access. Their absence in Beta 02 is therefore not proof that a purchase failed.

No page on this wiki promises that Beta 02 cash, XP, unlocks, or statistics will carry into Early Access. Treat test progression as temporary until an official statement says otherwise.

## Escalation Checklist

Before contacting support, capture the exact error, UTC time, selected region, entitlement state, system specifications, driver version, and steps that reproduce the problem. Remove email addresses, account tokens, QR codes, and Steam Guard details from screenshots.

## FAQ

**Should I reinstall when the server browser is empty?**
No. Select one region first and check official service status. Reinstalling cannot create server capacity or a missing Steam entitlement.

**Are the voice workarounds permanent fixes?**
No. They are developer-supplied Beta 02 workarounds and may become unnecessary after a service or build update.
