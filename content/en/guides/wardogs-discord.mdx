## Access Limits

Joining the server is not a beta key. For the August 21-23, 2026 Closed Beta, Steam Request Access offers a chance to be selected, while a pre-order guarantees entry under the published terms. A Discord role may help organize a community, but it does not replace the entitlement shown in the Steam library.

Legacy FirstLook account verification belongs to earlier testing history and should not be presented as a mandatory step for current Steam beta access. Follow only instructions that are current, clearly dated, and posted through official channels.

## Safety

Disable unsolicited direct messages if necessary and be skeptical of anyone offering keys, staff status, account recovery, or priority access. Real moderators do not need your password, Discord token, Steam Guard code, session cookie, recovery code, or remote desktop control.

Do not run attachments described as launchers, anti-cheat fixes, verification tools, or VOD codecs. Report impersonators through Discord's tools and the server moderation route. If an access announcement matters, verify it against Steam before signing in or paying.
