The three WARDOGS factions are **Valkyra, Lonestar, and Manticore**. They provide names for the three sides in the game's large-scale conflict, but no official source currently confirms unique bonuses, locked arsenals, or faction-specific balance traits.

## Three Factions

WARDOGS matches split up to 100 players across three teams. The approved faction names are Valkyra, Lonestar, and Manticore. Keep those names distinct from temporary team colors, squad labels, player-made clans, or map markers seen in pre-release footage.

A three-sided structure changes the battle even without asymmetric faction mechanics. Two opponents can pressure the same Control Zone from different routes, a leading team can become the focus of both rivals, and a fight between two groups can create an opening for the third. That strategic effect comes from the team format, not from an assumed faction perk.

## Confirmed Lore

The confirmed lore boundary is narrow. WARDOGS uses a derelict industrial mountain setting in Eastern Europe, and the three combatants have the names above. Official material checked for this guide does not establish a complete origin story, nationality, political ideology, named leadership, corporate ownership, or campaign chronology for each faction.

Do not infer lore from a logo, accent, uniform color, weapon in a screenshot, or the sound of a name. Those details may reflect art direction or build content without defining a permanent fictional history.

## Team Identity

At minimum, faction identity helps players understand which of three sides they are supporting in the Control Zone contest. Teamwork, local communication, cash decisions, transport, logistics, construction, destruction, and objective presence determine practical success under the currently described rules.

Pre-release videos may show blue, green, or other interface colors. Colors are useful for reading that recorded match, but they should not be promoted into official faction names or permanent palettes unless current game material explicitly connects them.

## Unknown Bonuses

No confirmed information gives Valkyra, Lonestar, or Manticore exclusive weapons, vehicles, starting cash, passive income, movement modifiers, spawn rules, role limits, progression tracks, or statistical advantages. There is also no supported basis for ranking one faction as best for beginners, infantry, armor, pilots, or logistics.

Until BULKHEAD, Team17, WARDOGS, or Steam publishes a faction systems breakdown, choose a team according to the live match and friends you want to join, not an unofficial tier list. Future announcements may deepen identity or keep factions mechanically even; this guide does not predict which direction development will take.
