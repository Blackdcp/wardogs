## Quick Answer

The three WARDOGS factions are **Lonestar, Valkyra, and Manticore**. Official lore gives each army a distinct identity in the fight for Kolchia and PV-1, but no source currently confirms unique statistical bonuses, locked arsenals, or faction-specific balance traits.

## Confirmed Facts

- The three named WARDOGS factions are Lonestar, Valkyra, and Manticore.
- Matches split up to 100 players across three teams.
- Official lore places the conflict in Kolchia around the resource PV-1.
- No official source confirms faction bonuses, passive traits, or exclusive gear.

## Three Factions

WARDOGS matches split up to 100 players across three teams. The official faction names are Lonestar, Valkyra, and Manticore. Keep those names distinct from temporary squad labels, player-made clans, or map markers seen in pre-release footage.

A three-sided structure changes the battle even without asymmetric faction mechanics. Two opponents can pressure the same Control Zone from different routes, a leading team can become the focus of both rivals, and a fight between two groups can create an opening for the third. That strategic effect comes from the team format, not from an assumed faction perk.

## Confirmed Lore

The official Top Questions announcement gives all three sides a short identity. **Lonestar** is described as the heavy-hitter of the Western paramilitary world. **Valkyra** aims to return the Soviet People's Republic to greatness. **Manticore** represents the Kingdom of Persia and is described as Tehran's shadow army.

Their battlefield is war-torn **Kolchia**, a fictional setting built around the struggle for **PV-1**, a rare resource said to have fuelled decades of Eurasian conflict. The same announcement says Kolchia contains multiple locations and points of interest designed for grounded military play. Beyond those published descriptions, named leaders, alliances, campaign chronology, and detailed political history remain unconfirmed.

## Team Identity

Faction identity tells players which of three sides they support in the Control Zone contest. Teamwork, local communication, cash decisions, transport, logistics, construction, destruction, and objective presence determine practical success under the currently described rules.

The official announcement associates blue with Lonestar, red with Valkyra, and green with Manticore. Those colors help players read the three-way battle, but a color or uniform seen in an Alpha clip should not be used to invent additional lore, permanent equipment restrictions, or a gameplay advantage.

## Unknown Bonuses

No confirmed information gives Valkyra, Lonestar, or Manticore exclusive weapons, vehicles, starting cash, passive income, movement modifiers, spawn rules, role limits, progression tracks, or statistical advantages. There is also no supported basis for ranking one faction as best for beginners, infantry, armor, pilots, or logistics.

Until BULKHEAD, Team17, WARDOGS, or Steam publishes a faction systems breakdown, choose a team according to its identity, the live match, and the friends you want to join, not an unofficial tier list. Future announcements may deepen identity or keep factions mechanically even; this guide does not predict which direction development will take.
