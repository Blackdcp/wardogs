## WakeUp's solo/duo V2: build, re-enter and test before sealing

Use this design when one or two players can supply, operate and repair a compact fire-support FOB. For a public reinforcement base that needs constant foot traffic, retain a normal entrance instead. [WakeUp's recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z7wMLQQtIIM) demonstrates the layout in the firing range; it is a build-along reference, not a measured live-match survival test.

| Prepare before leaving | What to check or write down |
| --- | --- |
| One transport vehicle, one building-material pallet, one ammunition pallet | This is the creator's opening load. Record the selected vehicle's accepted load and the purchased quantities; no specific truck model is established by the automatic caption |
| FOB, hammer and access to the demonstrated structures | Check the placement preview and resource requirement for Bremer walls, Hesco variants, indirect-fire shelter, recon tower, mortar pit and Talon before buying the entire load |
| Construction and repair reserve | Record resources before the shell, before weapons and after completion. Keep replacement material for the re-entry opening; one pallet in the recording is not a universal cost guarantee |
| Intended firing direction and supply approach | Mark both on the [map](/en/tools/map); choose the service side before placing walls |

The stages below use the video's relative positions and timestamps. Uneven terrain or a rejected placement is a reason to adjust the preview, not to force the same screenshot onto a different site.

## Ammunition handover and a low-stock return route

This compact layout needs a receiving route as well as a firing position. Keep the supply-side entrance clear, identify who accepts the ammunition and confirm the resource change after unloading. Use the [FOB handover checklist](/en/guides/wardogs-fob-guide) for ammunition versus building supplies; nearby pallets alone do not prove a working resupply. A buildable ammunition drop box and an assault ladder discussed by players are proposals, not recipes required by this design.

## Quick Answer

Choose a FOB layout for its job: reinforcement near a tower, dispersed survival under mortar fire, or protected logistics staging. Separate spawn, ammunition, repair and unloading paths so one hit does not stop every function.

## Confirmed Facts

The layouts below are tactical recommendations, not officially guaranteed safe distances or structure caps. Current building permissions, costs and supply requirements must be read in the client. Update 0.1.2 also changes rewards associated with supply abuse.

## Action Plan

1. **Orient and preview the perimeter — 0:44–3:17.** Face the FOB away from the intended Talon firing direction. Start the Bremer line beside it, slightly to the left. Snap the next wall, unsnap and offset it to the demonstrated halfway position; the creator repeats three sections on each side. Preview corners and close visible gaps before completing walls. If a corner overlaps, reposition it while it is still a preview.
2. **Build the central access — 3:37–4:59.** Place the indirect-fire shelter on the chosen extension side, then the recon tower with its ladder facing inward. Keep the working openings accessible while placing the next pieces. Walk from unloading side to shelter and ladder with the hammer before sealing anything.
3. **Add Hesco protection — 5:05–7:16.** Follow the video around the tower and shelter. Build from the side that leaves the hammer mark reachable; at 6:23 the creator demonstrates marks trapped between blocks. If the mark disappears, change side or reposition the unfinished piece rather than adding another wall over it.
4. **Place the fire-support side — 7:27–9:57.** Orient the mortar pit outward, then the upper Bremer/Hesco positions and the door facing the mortar. Finish the Talon while standing beside it: the demonstrated completion bug can push a player standing on top into the blocks.
5. **Test movement before final sealing — 10:08–14:35.** Enter and climb out of every side pocket; test shelter access, the stairs and the escape door. The door near the Talon is an exit, not the dependable normal entrance. In duo play, one player stays outside while the other checks the route.
6. **Test the actual weapon view — 16:18.** Check the intended low-angle Talon line before adding the last sandbags. If your own cover blocks it, change the cover or orientation. A sheltered position is not invulnerable to grenades, rockets or artillery.

Only finish the shell after the return procedure below works. The firing-range construction speed and the creator's illustrated mortar reach must not be used as match build-time or universal range values.

## Worked example

**Death and re-entry drill:** before committing the load, identify the Hesco above the indirect-fire shelter that you can reach from outside. The creator describes parachute re-entry or removing that block with a hammer, entering and rebuilding it. Plan a normal open entrance if you cannot use either route reliably. Do not deliberately spend a life to test it: have a teammate remain outside, or walk out and attempt the return while the opening is still recoverable.

| Record on one trial | Fill with your observation |
| --- | --- |
| Opening | Block type/position removed; screenshot before removal |
| Repair | Each removed or damaged Hesco, including the mortar-pit perimeter affected by reopening; quantity actually rebuilt |
| Cost | Build resources before/after; any cash actually spent replenishing them; time away from the weapon |
| Acceptance | Can you enter, reach the hammer marks, restore the opening and leave again? |

If the return consumes the repair reserve, leaves an unreachable block or traps the operator, stop sealing the layout and keep a service entrance. Save the completed layout and supply quantities in the [FOB planner](/en/tools/logistics-planner); it does not verify placement in the game.

## Common mistake

Walls that block your own loading, repair or evacuation are not an upgrade. Walk the route as a fresh spawn and drive the supply approach before spending on extra structures. Recheck after each attack because the enemy has learned the layout.
