## The official seasonal reset policy — and the Season 02 gap

**Yes, BULKHEAD has announced seasonal cash and XP resets.** In its [April 7 Early Access & Beyond video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PQvtvAvl-78&t=185s), the studio says that at a season's end cash and XP progression reset, remaining cash is automatically converted into Gold Bars, and Gold Bars and cosmetics stay on the account across seasons. This is a first-party policy statement, not a creator prediction. The same video describes permanent Player Skills as **planned**; do not assume those skills are live or earned today.

**Season 02 begins October 15, 2026.** Its [September teaser](https://steamcommunity.com/games/1867240/announcements/detail/677384059121304815) does not specify the transition hour, a separate Season 1 shutdown time, the conversion rate used at the boundary, a detailed item-by-item reset table, or whether every planned persistent system is implemented by then. The policy makes a seasonal reset the stated plan; it does not turn those missing operational details into facts. Use the [Season 02 guide](/en/guides/wardogs-season-2) for the dated announcement and update this ledger when BULKHEAD posts the transition notice.

## Asset-by-asset wipe checklist
The [official seasonal policy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PQvtvAvl-78&t=185s) establishes the first four outcomes below. A reset of progression is not evidence that every purchase fee is refunded.

| Asset | Announced rule / unresolved detail | Record before transition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cash | Resets; remaining cash automatically converts to Gold | Balance and timestamp; boundary conversion rate is not announced |
| Career and role XP | XP progression resets | Career and each role separately |
| Gold Bars | Persists across seasons | Gold balance; do not substitute a creator's historical quote |
| Cosmetics | Persists across seasons | Owned items |
| Paid unlock fees | Individual retention, repayment or refund treatment not specified in the reviewed transition material | Item, level gate and amount already paid |
| Steam achievements | Not specified by the cash/XP policy | Steam achievement state separately from game progression |

The announced policy preserves Gold Bars. For current exchange directions and rates, see the [Gold market](/en/gold-market); those live exchanges do **not** establish the season-end automatic cash-conversion rate, which remains unannounced here.

Keep the screenshots private if account details are visible. Record the next season's states before spending again. A missing item or unexpected conversion needs a dated support report, not repeated purchases to test it. Exact Season 02 transition time and rate remain unannounced.

### Buy an unlock now or keep cash for the transition?

Buy for a Season 1 task you can still use, and include the unlock fee, vendor purchase and replacement reserve in the [loadout budget](/en/tools/loadout-budget). The cash/XP reset policy does not settle whether a paid weapon unlock, attachment or stored item survives, is repaid or is refunded; keep the individual treatment in the checklist above unresolved.

Keeping cash also has a stated outcome: remaining cash automatically becomes Gold at season end. That does not establish which choice gives more Gold, because the boundary rate is still unspecified. Read the current quote using the [money guide](/en/guides/wardogs-money-guide), record Gold and cosmetics separately, and recheck the transition notice before assuming a purchase carries over.

## Quick Answer

Do not use the three-day Beta as a leveling-speed calculator for the live game. It was a compressed build designed to expose more weapons, vehicles, roles, and economy steps in a limited window. Recheck the current Early Access track before deciding which unlock route is fastest.

For old Beta-to-Early-Access carryover, do not assume test progress transferred without a migration statement. For **season-to-season** carryover, the studio did give a policy: cash and XP reset, while Gold Bars and cosmetics persist. Keep these two transitions separate; a confirmed policy does not establish the exact Season 02 reset hour or conversion rate.

## Confirmed Facts

The balancing update says the Closed Beta progression rate was deliberately compressed because the event lasted only three days. Players therefore reached equipment and vehicle gates much faster than intended for the live game. Its statement about the temporary main War Dog route and planned Driver destination describes the pre-launch state; the Season 1 changelog specifies the Heavy Tank at Driver 35 and Artillery Tank at Career 90.

That clarification resolves two common misunderstandings. First, a Beta level reached in a few hours does not establish the launch grind. Second, a tank shown inside one test menu does not prove its permanent unlock category. Treat screenshots and creator walkthroughs as dated build records.

The official Beta recap said progression and the wider metagame were still being revised ahead of Early Access. It did not provide a final XP table, permanent season length, or guaranteed transfer policy, so live menus and newer patch notes supersede its test values.

### Driver and Support XP: choose a task, then check the credited track

Choose the desired unlock before choosing a job. Published Season 1 examples are **Dune Buggy at Driver 8**, **Heavy Tank at Driver 35** and **Large Hammer at Support 8**; Artillery Tank requires **Career 90**. Recheck the current menu before paying.

- **Driver task:** agree on a pickup and drop-off with the squad, transport the group along a usable route and preserve the vehicle for the return. Record Driver XP, Career XP and cash before and after the task.
- **Support task:** ask a staffed FOB what material or construction work is needed, deliver the requested supplies and help complete that job. Use the [FOB guide](/en/guides/wardogs-fob-guide) and record Support XP, Career XP and cash separately.

These are useful team tasks to measure, not confirmed XP formulas. A cash payout for friendly transport does not prove an equal Driver XP award; a completed delivery does not establish a separate cash payout. The [role route tool](/en/tools/progression-route) shows selected published gates, while the current client establishes what your task actually credited.
