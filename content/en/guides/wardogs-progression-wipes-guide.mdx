## The official seasonal reset policy — and the Season 02 gap

**Yes, BULKHEAD has announced seasonal cash and XP resets.** In its [April 7 Early Access & Beyond video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PQvtvAvl-78&t=185s), the studio says that at a season's end cash and XP progression reset, remaining cash is automatically converted into Gold Bars, and Gold Bars and cosmetics stay on the account across seasons. This is a first-party policy statement, not a creator prediction. The same video describes permanent Player Skills as **planned**; do not assume those skills are live or earned today.

**Season 02 begins October 15, 2026.** Its [September teaser](https://steamcommunity.com/games/1867240/announcements/detail/677384059121304815) does not specify the transition hour, a separate Season 1 shutdown time, the conversion rate used at the boundary, a detailed item-by-item reset table, or whether every planned persistent system is implemented by then. The policy makes a seasonal reset the stated plan; it does not turn those missing operational details into facts. Use the [Season 02 guide](/en/guides/wardogs-season-2) for the dated announcement and update this ledger when BULKHEAD posts the transition notice.

## Season 1 checkpoint - September 17, 2026

**Current build note:** WARDOGS is live in Season 1 Early Access. Patch 0.11 is the latest official patch announcement; the published September 14 maintenance window has passed. Use the live client and the official Steam feed for service status and values that can change.

The official Season 1 curve now publishes career XP checkpoints: level 6 at 9,250 XP, 10 at 35,000, 15 at 110,000, 20 at 250,000, 25 at 325,000, 30 at 400,000, 35 at 500,000, 40 at 650,000, 45 at 800,000 and 50 at 1,000,000. Item order differs by role and season; do not use Beta unlock tables as the live answer.

## September 17 live-build note

WARDOGS Early Access is live. The Patch 0.11 notice confirms fixes for multiple cash exploits and warns that repeated abuse, exploit tutorials, or XP farming can lead to account resets or bans. That **punitive account reset** is separate from the **seasonal cash/XP policy** stated in BULKHEAD's April video; neither source gives a permanent season-length calendar.

WARDOGS progression in the August Closed Beta was intentionally accelerated for a short test. The [Season 1 changelog](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1867240/view/701027323413004455) specifies the named tanks separately: **Heavy Tank unlocks at Driver level 35**, while **Artillery Tank unlocks at Career level 90**. Old Beta thresholds and menu screenshots remain historical records.

## Quick Answer

Do not use the three-day Beta as a leveling-speed calculator for the live game. It was a compressed build designed to expose more weapons, vehicles, roles, and economy steps in a limited window. Recheck the current Early Access track before deciding which unlock route is fastest.

For old Beta-to-Early-Access carryover, do not assume test progress transferred without a migration statement. For **season-to-season** carryover, the studio did give a policy: cash and XP reset, while Gold Bars and cosmetics persist. Keep these two transitions separate; a confirmed policy does not establish the exact Season 02 reset hour or conversion rate.

## Confirmed Facts

The balancing update says the Closed Beta progression rate was deliberately compressed because the event lasted only three days. Players therefore reached equipment and vehicle gates much faster than intended for the live game. Its statement about the temporary main War Dog route and planned Driver destination describes the pre-launch state; the Season 1 changelog specifies the Heavy Tank at Driver 35 and Artillery Tank at Career 90.

That clarification resolves two common misunderstandings. First, a Beta level reached in a few hours does not establish the launch grind. Second, a tank shown inside one test menu does not prove its permanent unlock category. Treat screenshots and creator walkthroughs as dated build records.

The official Beta recap said progression and the wider metagame were still being revised ahead of Early Access. It did not provide a final XP table, permanent season length, or guaranteed transfer policy, so live menus and newer patch notes supersede its test values.

## How the Six Role Tracks Should Be Read

The tested progression design groups unlocks around six role-based tracks rather than one universal weapon ladder. The useful planning principle is to perform the job connected to the equipment you want: transport and vehicle work should feed Driver-oriented progression, while combat and support activity belong to their relevant role paths.

Other track details, XP sources, thresholds, cross-track sharing, and reward order remain build-sensitive. Do not publish a permanent “best track” ranking from the Beta. A track can change value when a vehicle moves category, an XP reward is rebalanced, or a later build changes the unlock order.

Before committing to a route in Early Access:

1. Open every live progression tab and record its first practical unlock.
2. Check whether XP is earned by role actions, match result, objective play, or all three.
3. Confirm whether switching jobs mid-match splits or preserves credit.
4. Compare the cash cost of using an unlock with the time needed to reach it.
5. Recheck the page after the first balance patch.

## Heavy Tank and Artillery Tank Tracks

During the Closed Beta, tanks appeared in the main War Dog/Career track as a temporary testing arrangement. That is historical context. These are two named unlocks on different tracks, not a universal tank level.

## Will Progress Be Wiped?

No official source currently promises that Closed Beta progress carries into Early Access. Test builds normally use temporary data, but that general expectation is not a substitute for a WARDOGS statement. The accurate player-facing wording is:

- Beta cash, XP, unlocks, statistics, and settings may be reset.
- No complete public Beta-to-Early-Access migration table was found in the reviewed official sources.
- BULKHEAD's stated seasonal policy resets cash and XP progression; remaining cash converts to Gold Bars, while Gold Bars and cosmetics persist.
- The exact Season 02 boundary time, conversion rate and complete item-by-item treatment still need a newer transition notice.
- Purchased game ownership is separate from temporary in-game progression.

Avoid two opposite errors. Do not promise Beta carryover because Steam still shows a Playtest entry, and do not say seasonal wipes are unconfirmed when the developer has already described them. Also do not invent a precise October 15 reset hour from the teaser alone.

## Cash, Gold Bars, XP, and Unlocks

Cash is part of the match-to-match economy described by WARDOGS, while XP and role tracks control access to equipment. The [official video at 03:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PQvtvAvl-78&t=185s) makes the persistence boundary clear: seasonal cash and XP reset; Gold Bars and cosmetics carry across seasons. It also presents Player Skills as future permanent progression, not proof that the feature is already live. A Beta exchange rate or reward may have been tuned to expose systems quickly rather than represent the current economy.
