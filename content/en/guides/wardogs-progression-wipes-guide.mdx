WARDOGS progression in the August Closed Beta was intentionally accelerated for a short test. The developer has since clarified that Early Access progression should be slower and that tanks will move from the temporary main War Dog path into the **Driver** role track. What has not been confirmed is equally important: there is no published promise that Beta cash, XP, unlocks, statistics, or settings will carry into Early Access, and no complete public season or wipe schedule exists yet.

## Quick Answer

Do not use the three-day Beta as a leveling-speed calculator for September 10. It was a compressed build designed to expose more weapons, vehicles, roles, and economy steps in a limited window. Learn how the tracks relate to jobs, but wait for the Early Access build before deciding which unlock route is fastest.

For carryover, use the conservative answer: **assume test progress can be reset** until WARDOGS publishes an explicit migration statement. That is different from claiming a wipe is confirmed. The current evidence supports uncertainty, not a made-up reset date.

## Confirmed Facts

The balancing update says the Closed Beta progression rate was deliberately compressed because the event lasted only three days. Players therefore reached equipment and vehicle gates much faster than intended for the live game. The same update explains that tanks were temporarily placed in the main War Dog progression route for testing and are planned to move to the Driver route in Early Access.

That clarification resolves two common misunderstandings. First, a Beta level reached in a few hours does not establish the launch grind. Second, a tank shown inside one test menu does not prove its permanent unlock category. Treat screenshots and creator walkthroughs as dated build records.

The official Beta recap also says progression and the wider metagame are active areas of work before Early Access. It does not provide a final XP table, permanent season length, or guaranteed transfer policy.

## What Players Search For

Players usually need three answers: whether Closed Beta progress carries into Early Access, whether Early Access or future seasons will wipe progress, and which role track unlocks tanks. The first two remain unconfirmed; the developer has answered the third by moving tanks to the Driver route.

## How to Use This Guide

Read the confirmed statements first, then use the role-track and launch checklists below to test the live build. Any XP threshold, price, unlock order, or reset rule that lacks an official launch source remains build-sensitive.

## How the Six Role Tracks Should Be Read

The tested progression design groups unlocks around six role-based tracks rather than one universal weapon ladder. The useful planning principle is to perform the job connected to the equipment you want: transport and vehicle work should feed Driver-oriented progression, while combat and support activity belong to their relevant role paths.

Exact track names, XP sources, thresholds, cross-track sharing, and reward order remain build-sensitive. Do not publish a permanent “best track” ranking from the Beta. A track can change value when a vehicle moves category, an XP reward is rebalanced, or a launch build changes the unlock order.

Before committing to a route in Early Access:

1. Open every live progression tab and record its first practical unlock.
2. Check whether XP is earned by role actions, match result, objective play, or all three.
3. Confirm whether switching jobs mid-match splits or preserves credit.
4. Compare the cash cost of using an unlock with the time needed to reach it.
5. Recheck the page after the first balance patch.

## Tanks and the Driver Track

During the Closed Beta, tanks appeared in the main War Dog track as a temporary testing arrangement. The developer update says they will move to the Driver track for Early Access. That means players interested in tanks should prepare for transport, vehicle handling, logistics, repair, and objective support to matter more than simply grinding a generic level.

The statement does not reveal the final tank level, purchase price, exact XP sources, or whether every armored vehicle shares one gate. ## Will Progress Be Wiped?

No official source currently promises that Closed Beta progress carries into Early Access. Test builds normally use temporary data, but that general expectation is not a substitute for a WARDOGS statement. The accurate player-facing wording is:

- Beta cash, XP, unlocks, statistics, and settings may be reset.
- An Early Access launch reset has not been given a public date or migration table.
- Future seasonal resets, if any, have not been fully defined.
- Purchased game ownership is separate from temporary in-game progression.

Avoid two opposite errors. Do not promise carryover because Steam still shows a Playtest entry, and do not announce a specific wipe because another extraction or persistent-economy game uses one.

## Cash, Gold Bars, XP, and Unlocks

Cash is part of the match-to-match economy described by WARDOGS, while XP and role tracks control access to equipment. Gold Bars, market systems, and any long-term account layer need to be checked against the live build before assigning permanent value. A Beta price or reward may have been tuned to expose systems quickly rather than represent the Early Access economy.

Use the [money guide](/guides/wardogs-money-guide) for current earning routes, but keep cash planning separate from XP planning. A profitable support run may help both systems, yet the exact payout and progression credit can change independently.

## Confirmed vs Unknown

**Confirmed:** Beta progression was deliberately accelerated; the live pace should be slower; tanks were temporarily in the main War Dog path and are planned for the Driver track; progression and metagame work continues before Early Access.

**Unknown:** final XP thresholds, exact unlock order, complete role labels, carryover from Beta, the first Early Access wipe, season length, reset scope, and whether any currency survives a future season.

## FAQ

**What role should tank players level?** The developer says tanks are moving to Driver for Early Access. Verify the live Driver track before spending cash or assuming a Beta unlock level.

**Can a creator video confirm wipe rules?** A creator can preserve a developer statement or show one build, but only an official WARDOGS notice can define account-wide carryover and reset policy.
