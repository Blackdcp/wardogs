## September 13 live-build note

WARDOGS Early Access is live. The Patch 0.11 notice confirms fixes for multiple cash exploits and warns that repeated abuse, exploit tutorials, or XP farming can lead to account resets or bans. That enforcement warning is not a general seasonal wipe schedule, which remains unpublished.

WARDOGS progression in the August Closed Beta was intentionally accelerated for a short test. The developer had said Early Access progression would be slower and that tanks would move from the temporary main War Dog path into the **Driver** role track. Recheck both against the live build before relying on an old threshold or menu screenshot.

## Quick Answer

Do not use the three-day Beta as a leveling-speed calculator for the live game. It was a compressed build designed to expose more weapons, vehicles, roles, and economy steps in a limited window. Recheck the current Early Access track before deciding which unlock route is fastest.

For carryover, use the conservative answer: **assume test progress can be reset** until WARDOGS publishes an explicit migration statement. That is different from claiming a wipe is confirmed. The current evidence supports uncertainty, not a made-up reset date.

## Confirmed Facts

The balancing update says the Closed Beta progression rate was deliberately compressed because the event lasted only three days. Players therefore reached equipment and vehicle gates much faster than intended for the live game. The same pre-launch update said tanks were temporarily placed in the main War Dog route and were intended for the Driver route; the current in-game track is authoritative.

That clarification resolves two common misunderstandings. First, a Beta level reached in a few hours does not establish the launch grind. Second, a tank shown inside one test menu does not prove its permanent unlock category. Treat screenshots and creator walkthroughs as dated build records.

The official Beta recap said progression and the wider metagame were still being revised ahead of Early Access. It did not provide a final XP table, permanent season length, or guaranteed transfer policy, so live menus and newer patch notes supersede its test values.

## How the Six Role Tracks Should Be Read

The tested progression design groups unlocks around six role-based tracks rather than one universal weapon ladder. The useful planning principle is to perform the job connected to the equipment you want: transport and vehicle work should feed Driver-oriented progression, while combat and support activity belong to their relevant role paths.

Exact track names, XP sources, thresholds, cross-track sharing, and reward order remain build-sensitive. Do not publish a permanent “best track” ranking from the Beta. A track can change value when a vehicle moves category, an XP reward is rebalanced, or a launch build changes the unlock order.

Before committing to a route in Early Access:

1. Open every live progression tab and record its first practical unlock.
2. Check whether XP is earned by role actions, match result, objective play, or all three.
3. Confirm whether switching jobs mid-match splits or preserves credit.
4. Compare the cash cost of using an unlock with the time needed to reach it.
5. Recheck the page after the first balance patch.

## Tanks and the Driver Track

During the Closed Beta, tanks appeared in the main War Dog track as a temporary testing arrangement. A pre-launch developer update identified the Driver track as the intended destination. Players interested in tanks should verify the live route and expect transport, vehicle handling, logistics, repair, and objective support to matter more than simply grinding a generic level.

The statement does not reveal the final tank level, purchase price, exact XP sources, or whether every armored vehicle shares one gate. ## Enforcement Resets Are Not Wipes

Patch 0.11 introduced a penalty that looks like a wipe but is not one. Players who abuse a cash exploit or bug can have **cash and XP reset to 0** along with a timed ban. XP farming and publishing exploit tutorials can bring a permanent ban, and BULKHEAD says it tracks which servers an account joins to identify farming servers.

The distinction matters for planning. A seasonal or Early Access wipe would affect everyone; an enforcement reset affects one account because of a specific behaviour. If your balance or level suddenly drops to zero, review whether you were in a server that specialised in abnormal payouts before assuming a global wipe happened.

No seasonal wipe has been announced. Treat any claim that "progress resets every season" as unconfirmed until an official note says it.

## Will Progress Be Wiped?

No official source currently promises that Closed Beta progress carries into Early Access. Test builds normally use temporary data, but that general expectation is not a substitute for a WARDOGS statement. The accurate player-facing wording is:

- Beta cash, XP, unlocks, statistics, and settings may be reset.
- No complete public Beta-to-Early-Access migration table was found in the reviewed official sources.
- Future seasonal resets, if any, have not been fully defined.
- Purchased game ownership is separate from temporary in-game progression.

Avoid two opposite errors. Do not promise carryover because Steam still shows a Playtest entry, and do not announce a specific wipe because another extraction or persistent-economy game uses one.

## Cash, Gold Bars, XP, and Unlocks

Cash is part of the match-to-match economy described by WARDOGS, while XP and role tracks control access to equipment. Gold Bars, market systems, and any long-term account layer need to be checked against the live build before assigning permanent value. A Beta price or reward may have been tuned to expose systems quickly rather than represent the Early Access economy.

Use the [money guide](/en/guides/wardogs-money-guide) for current earning routes, but keep cash planning separate from XP planning. A profitable support run may help both systems, yet the exact payout and progression credit can change independently.
