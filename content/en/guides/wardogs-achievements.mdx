## Quick Answer

**WARDOGS has 10 Steam achievements.** The [official Steam list](https://steamcommunity.com/stats/1867240/achievements) publishes conditions for nine. It displays the name **That was rude** without a description, so its unlock condition is not publicly disclosed there. The table below paraphrases Steam's visible requirements; the planning column is our tactical advice, not an official method or a guaranteed unlock route.

## When an achievement does not trigger

- **Public condition:** compare the required event and accounting window with your result. Fat Stacks is net profit in one life, not the match total; Big Spender concerns one loadout, not purchases across several lives. Use the [loadout budget tool](/en/tools/loadout-budget) to plan that kit and your reserve, then verify the purchase in the live vendor. The tool does not read Steam progress or certify an unlock.
- **Hidden condition:** for **That was rude**, the public page supplies no requirement. A failed community recipe cannot prove a broken trigger; keep it as an unverified lead, not an official route.
- **Expected result, still locked:** record the achievement name, server/build, date, relevant life or match boundaries, visible game result and Steam state, including when each was checked. If they differ, record a possible reporting delay or trigger issue without choosing a cause. No fixed synchronization wait or guaranteed restart fix is established here; preserve unclear duration and payout rules as unknowns.

The cash/XP season-reset policy does not establish Steam achievement treatment. Track Steam state separately before/after a transition. A failed trigger needs reproducible evidence; it is not proof that achievements were wiped.
