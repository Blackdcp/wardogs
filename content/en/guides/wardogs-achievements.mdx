## Quick Answer

**WARDOGS has 10 Steam achievements.** The [official Steam list](https://steamcommunity.com/stats/1867240/achievements) publishes conditions for nine. It displays the name **That was rude** without a description, so its unlock condition is not publicly disclosed there. The table below paraphrases Steam's visible requirements; the planning column is our tactical advice, not an official method or a guaranteed unlock route.

## All 10 Achievements

| Steam name | Public condition, paraphrased | Practical planning, not an official method |
| --- | --- | --- |
| This is WARDOGS | Finish the tutorial. | Complete the tutorial before treating live matches as your first training session. |
| Ricochet | Eliminate an enemy with a bullet that ricochets. | Look for ordinary combat opportunities near hard cover; Steam gives no required weapon or bounce angle. |
| Fat Stacks | Make $100,000 net profit during one life. | Pick a useful team role you can sustain and preserve that life; compare income with the cost of your kit. |
| That was rude | **Hidden:** Steam shows no description. | No official condition is available on the public page, so do not plan around an unverified solution. |
| Long Shot | Eliminate an enemy from beyond 950 m. | Choose a long sightline and a loadout suited to distance; Steam does not specify a map, gun or optic. |
| Top Dog | End a match with the highest cash earnings among all teams. | Contribute throughout the match through objectives or support and check the final cash ranking. |
| CZ Survivor | Spend 60 minutes in the Control Zone without dying. | Favor covered positions, teammates and safe repositioning. Steam does not explain whether time must be continuous or span one match. |
| Do Not Resuscitate | Eliminate an enemy with a defibrillator charge. | Equip the tool when available and attempt this only in a normal, safe engagement; Steam names no unlock level or price. |
| Clean Sweep | Win while both opposing teams finish with zero points. | Coordinate Control Zone defense from the start; this is a team-wide match result, not a solo kill target. |
| Big Spender | Buy a single loadout costing $100,000. | Verify the total in the current vendor before purchasing and keep enough cash for later lives. It is distinct from profit. |

## Plan Without Inventing a Grind

Steam states the goals, not a fastest route, expected playtime or reward payout. For the two cash milestones, keep **net profit in one life** separate from **cost of one loadout**. The [money guide](/en/guides/wardogs-money-guide) explains the broader cash loop; it does not guarantee an achievement payout. For Top Dog and Clean Sweep, objective play and a coordinated [squad plan](/en/guides/wardogs-squad-guide) matter more than a personal kill count.

For distance and ricochet goals, use the current [weapons and loadouts guide](/en/guides/wardogs-best-weapons-loadouts) to choose equipment without assuming a fixed damage or bounce formula. For CZ Survivor, read the [map guide](/en/guides/wardogs-map) before committing to a position. The [medic guide](/en/guides/wardogs-medic-revive-guide) helps you prepare a defibrillator role, but Steam's achievement text alone does not specify how the tool is unlocked or how its charge behaves.

**Do not use staged kills, XP-farming servers, economy exploits or third-party unlock tools as an achievement strategy.** The table is a planning aid for ordinary play. A displayed Steam percentage is a moving snapshot, so no completion rates are fixed here.

## FAQ

**Is there an eleventh secret achievement?** Steam lists **10 total**. That was rude is one of those ten; its public row has no description.

**How do I unlock That was rude?** The public Steam page does not say. Community theories may be useful leads, but they are not an official condition and are not repeated as fact here.

**Does CZ Survivor require one uninterrupted hour in one match?** Steam says 60 minutes in the CZ without dying but does not clarify whether the time is continuous or may cross matches. Check progress in your Steam account rather than assuming a timer rule.

**Can I earn these on community servers?** The achievements page does not state a server restriction or confirm community-server eligibility. Check the current game and Steam behavior before building a plan around a particular server.
