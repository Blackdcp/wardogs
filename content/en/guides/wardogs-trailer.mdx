The official starting point is Team17's WARDOGS reveal trailer below. It introduces the large-scale combined-arms identity, but current dates, platforms, access terms, and requirements should be taken from the official store listing rather than inferred from footage.

## Reveal Trailer

<OfficialVideo id="hVtmnaUCpuQ" title="WARDOGS Reveal Trailer" />

The reveal presents infantry combat alongside vehicles, fortified positions, destruction, and a wide battlefield. Its role is to establish tone and intended scope. It is not a frame-by-frame manual, a final graphics comparison, or proof that every visible interface element and asset will appear unchanged in Early Access.

Team17 is the publisher and BULKHEAD is the developer. That attribution distinguishes this video from third-party explainers and reuploads that may use "official" in a title without being published by an authorized source.

## Gameplay Footage

Later official gameplay material and authorized Alpha creator footage provide longer examples of Control Zone movement, squad communication, transport, revives, construction, supply work, and vehicle pressure. Those videos can make the match rhythm easier to understand than a short trailer, but they answer a different question: what did a particular pre-release build look like in play?

Use clear labels when comparing them. An official publisher trailer, an official developer broadcast, and a creator's Closed Alpha session carry different levels of control and verification. A dramatic moment in any video does not automatically define a permanent mechanic.

## What Is Confirmed

Current official product information confirms a Windows Steam release, up to 100 players divided across three teams, a randomized 2x2 km Control Zone within a much larger map, persistent cash, vehicles, logistics, construction and destruction, and local voice communication. The first team to reach 100 points wins the main contest.

The trailer should not be used to invent a console version, exact launch time, faction bonus, weapon count, vehicle roster, or test entitlement. Those claims require explicit current text from Team17, BULKHEAD, WARDOGS, or Steam.

## Build Caveats

Pre-release footage can preserve old lighting, effects, animations, menus, performance behavior, objective markers, or balancing. Cuts and camera choices also make it unsuitable for measuring match length, travel time, server stability, or the frequency of specific vehicles.

When revisiting older video, note its publisher and build context, then check the Steam page for present facts. This keeps the trailer useful as a visual introduction without turning a changing development snapshot into a permanent specification.
