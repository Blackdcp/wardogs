## Quick Answer

Plan Havoc versus CIWS around the server build, sight lines and support crew. A hotfix headline does not supply a universal kill time. Check IR Rangefinder vendor availability separately from already owned inventory.

## Confirmed Facts

The October official announcements discuss anti-air and IR Rangefinder changes. CIWS is the catalogue term; some notices use CWIS. Exact combat results depend on the build, range, obstruction, damage state and pilot movement.

## Action Plan

1. Check the server and client build against the announcement.
2. Inspect the current vendor and owned inventory separately.
3. Place CIWS with a visible engagement lane, then inspect hills and structures.
4. Allocate ammunition, repair and an observer before relying on the position.
5. After contact, record range, angle and result before changing placement.

## Worked example

If a Havoc survives one pass, the result alone does not prove a failed hotfix. Check whether the aircraft crossed behind a ridge, whether the CIWS had ammunition and whether it fired for the whole engagement. Move or support the position before buying a second system.

## Common mistake

A removed purchase option does not establish deletion of owned IR Rangefinders. Do not publish pre-hotfix damage or time-to-kill as a current measured result without matching conditions.
