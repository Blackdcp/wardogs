**Treat the Havoc versus CIWS interaction as current-build anti-air planning. Confirm whether the server has the hotfix, place CIWS for line of sight, do not assume old kill times, and keep IR Rangefinder ownership separate from vendor availability.**

## Action Plan

### Check the server build

A region that has not restarted can differ from the published target state.

### Separate vendor and inventory

A disabled vendor purchase does not automatically prove every owned item disappeared.

### Place for line of sight

CIWS value drops if hills, buildings or FOB clutter block the engagement lane.

### Pair AA with logistics

An anti-air piece without repair, ammo and spotters becomes a one-time deterrent.

### Update old advice

Do not repeat pre-hotfix Havoc timing as if it were current.
