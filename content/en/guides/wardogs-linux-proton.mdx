## Quick Answer

WARDOGS is **not officially supported on Linux as of the September 25 developer update**. BULKHEAD has said it is working on official Linux Proton compatibility, but it has not named a supported Proton version, a date, or a Steam Deck status. If Linux compatibility is essential for your purchase, wait for a clear update on the official Steam page or from BULKHEAD.

In its [2026-09-25 update to the pinned Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1867240/discussions/0/588436698284962639), the developer explicitly said it had no additional compatibility information or timeline to share. Proton remains a goal, not an announced release date or a newly supported build.

This is deliberately narrower than a claim that the game can never start on a particular distribution, desktop PC, or Steam Deck. A player report, one beta build, or a launcher workaround can change quickly and does not become an official support promise. This page records the publisher's current position rather than turning community testing into a guarantee.

## Confirmed Facts

- WARDOGS is currently announced for Windows PC on Steam.
- BULKHEAD says Linux is not officially supported at launch.
- BULKHEAD says official Linux Proton compatibility is being worked on.
- No launch-day Linux commitment, supported Proton build, or support date is published.
- No official Steam Deck Verified or Playable status is published.
- Steam and BULKHEAD are the places to check when the platform scope changes.

The Windows hardware targets on Steam are still useful only for the Windows version. ## What This Does Not Confirm

Official Proton work does not tell us which Proton release will be supported, whether every Linux distribution will work, whether a Steam Deck performance target exists, or when a Steam compatibility label will appear. It also does not confirm a native Linux build. Until an official update names those details, they remain unknown.

Do not treat a community report of a successful beta session as proof that the commercial build will work the same way. Test applications, server configurations, anti-cheat integrations, patches, and storefront builds can differ. A report can be useful feedback for the developer, but it is not a purchase guarantee for other players.

## Safe Decision Before Buying

If you own a Windows PC, use the official Steam requirements and the [WARDOGS Download Guide](/en/guides/wardogs-download). If your only target device is Linux or Steam Deck, the safest decision is to wait until the official support language changes or Steam publishes a compatibility status.

Do not download unofficial launchers, replace game files, bypass anti-cheat, or follow instructions that claim to make an unsupported multiplayer client "safe." Those steps can create a security or account risk and cannot convert an unsupported environment into an officially supported one. Keep your Steam client and game files intact and use official support channels for a real issue.

## What to Watch Next

A meaningful update should identify at least one concrete item: official Linux support at a named milestone, an official Proton compatibility target, a verified Steam Deck result, or a clear change to the Steam storefront. A repost, screenshot, or creator test without an official origin is not enough to change this guide's status.

Check the WARDOGS Steam page and the official Steam Community updates before installing or buying specifically for Linux.
