## Quick Answer

Counter mortar pressure by breaking the firing loop: observe the origin, separate valuable FOB functions, protect resupply and relocate predictable assets. Choose L81 or SPH-2 based on crew, supply and exposure, not one universal meta ranking.

## Confirmed Facts

Community complaints and press coverage establish a balance discussion, not exact live blast damage. L81 and SPH-2 have different data and firing modes in the calculator. A suggested camera or splash nerf is not a shipped change until patch evidence confirms it.

## Action Plan

1. Select the correct map and calibrate against a known horizontal distance.
2. Mark firing point and target, then select the actual weapon and firing mode.
3. Check estimated range, bearing and any missing height data.
4. Fire a single observed ranging shot before a limited volley.
5. Log the impact correction and move when the enemy can locate you.

## Worked example

If repeated impacts hit the same supply area, first move vulnerable supplies and restore a covered delivery route. An observer can then search the likely firing direction while defenders spread out. Blind counterfire at an guessed origin consumes ammunition without confirming suppression.

## Common mistake

Do not apply one elevation rule to every trajectory: a higher target distance can require a higher mil in low arc and a lower mil in high arc. Follow the new solution for the selected weapon/mode and stop when the input is outside the supported table.
