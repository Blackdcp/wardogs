You do not need legacy FirstLook Discord verification for the current WARDOGS Closed Beta route. Steam now handles the relevant entitlement through Request Access or a qualifying pre-order; Discord membership and roles are community features.

## Current Need

For August 21-23, 2026, a Steam access request gives a player a chance to be selected, while a pre-order guarantees beta entry under the published terms. In both cases, the playable entitlement should be associated with the Steam account used for that action.

The official Discord can provide announcements, discussion, and troubleshooting. Joining it does not create a Steam license, and no current requirement says that linking or verifying Discord is necessary to make a valid Steam entitlement work.

## Legacy Steps

Older FirstLook-era registration allowed players to sign up through Discord or Steam and could use community verification to organize testing. That history explains search queries about linking an account, opening a dashboard, or waiting for a role. It should be described as a legacy flow, not rebuilt into current instructions.

Do not invent the names of old buttons, required channels, role colors, approval times, or account-link sequence. The historical interface is not available as a current, verified procedure, and following stale screenshots can expose an account to lookalike pages.

## Steam/Discord

Keep the two systems conceptually separate. Steam controls store ownership, beta selection, library entitlements, installation, and transaction support. Discord controls server membership, messages, roles, moderation, and community permissions. A successful action in one system does not automatically prove a particular state in the other.

Use the official invite at `discord.com/invite/playwardogs` for community access. Use Steam App 1867240 for the current game and test route. If an official future campaign requires account linking, follow the newly dated instructions from those channels rather than this historical summary.

## Troubleshooting

If Steam access is missing, confirm the signed-in account, request or purchase history, email notices, and library state. Contact Steam Support for entitlement or payment problems. If a Discord role or channel is missing, read the current server rules and support guidance and allow for moderation or onboarding delays.

Never give a moderator your password, Steam Guard code, Discord token, session cookie, or recovery codes. Do not run a verification executable or approve an unfamiliar OAuth request. A legitimate community check should not require secrets that allow another person to control your account.
