## Quick Answer

Season 2 is announced for October 15, 2026. Developers also describe four planned weapons and weather changes. Keep announced plans, released patch notes and measured client behavior separate when preparing your loadout.

## Confirmed Facts

Update 0.1.2 points to new map conditions, equipment and balance work. The developer interview adds one weapon per Assault, Medic, Support and Recon, plus rain/fog presentation. Full item names, values, launch hour and a Season 3 date are not established.

## Action Plan

1. Check the announcement date and whether it describes a plan or a shipped update.
2. For weapons, record name, role, unlock gate, price and source separately.
3. For the transition, check cash/XP, Gold and cosmetics separately.
4. After patching, verify old settings and loadouts against the client.
5. Retire old assumptions when a dated official correction appears.

## Worked example

A rain teaser can justify preparing visibility settings; it cannot establish tire grip, sound penalties or an FPS figure. A planned new rifle does not provide a damage table. Keep the current working loadout until a published item record or client evidence supports replacement.

## Common mistake

Do not turn an illustrative season length into a fixed schedule. A future 1.0 wipe discussion also cannot replace the current seasonal retention policy. Check the wipe and market guides for purchase decisions.

## October 9: developer interview update

Four new weapons are planned: one each for Assault, Medic, Support and Recon. The developer interview at [1:28:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=liRK9si1Ubo&t=5315s) is more specific than the teaser. This is an announced plan, not a verified live inventory. At [1:28:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=liRK9si1Ubo&t=5328s), automatic captions identify the Recon prototype as M14; the speaker does not recall its in-game name. Final names, prices, unlock gates and damage values remain unverified. Do not buy a loadout around an assumed M40 or a community teaser guess.

At [1:26:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=liRK9si1Ubo&t=5181s), developers discuss rain, fog, wet equipment and drying indoors. These are planned weather details, not a measured performance guarantee or proof that weather changes mid-match. At [46:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=liRK9si1Ubo&t=2764s), they explain that seasons have no fixed duration; there is no confirmed Season 3 date.
