## Quick Answer

The best WARDOGS loadout is the cheapest complete kit that lets you perform a defined job. Start with the distance you expect to fight, then choose a weapon family, compatible ammunition and magazines, enough storage, protection, and one role tool. Do not copy a permanent tier list: weapon prices, damage, recoil, unlocks, and attachment support are **build-sensitive**, and the final Early Access values are not confirmed.

## Confirmed Facts

Official WARDOGS pages confirm a combined-arms game with persistent cash, progression, infantry weapons, vehicles, logistics, and large three-faction battles. The official Team17 press kit also shows the M4 and Super-45 names in gameplay. It does not publish a final damage, recoil, price, or unlock table, so those values remain dated observations until the live build can be checked.

Players usually want a best gun, a low-cost starter kit, the right weapon for Medic or Recon, and a loadout that will not waste cash after one death. Those are different questions. This guide answers them by role, range, ammunition, storage, and replacement cost instead of forcing every weapon into one universal ranking.

## Choose a job before a gun

An assault player needs a controllable general-purpose rifle and enough ammunition to remain useful while moving between Control Zones. A close-quarters player values handling and rapid follow-up shots but must plan a route that avoids long exposed lanes. A support player needs sustained fire and teammates who can carry or deliver ammunition. A marksman should protect sightlines and identify targets rather than abandoning the squad to chase distant shots. Anti-vehicle equipment is a team resource, not a substitute for an infantry primary.

This role-first method is more durable than declaring one Alpha weapon "S-tier." The official game description confirms large combined-arms battles, logistics, vehicles, persistent cash and progression; it does not publish a final damage table. Creator footage helps identify names and workflows, but every numerical claim still belongs to the captured build.

## Reliable rifle starting point

For an adaptable infantry kit, compare assault rifles such as the M4, AK74, AK12, A-91, HK433, FAL, G3A3, HK416 and Mk 14 EBR in the [full weapons catalogue](/en/items/weapons). Look at ammunition family, observed fire modes, progression note, weight and observed test price together. A familiar name is not proof that its real-world performance is reproduced in game.

Choose the rifle you can control in short bursts and support with compatible magazines. Keep enough money for protection and recovery. If your squad expects repeated indoor fights, a shorter weapon may be easier to manage; if the route crosses open ground, a rifle with a clear optic and disciplined semi-auto fire may be the better complete kit.

## Close-range and medic kits

SMGs such as the MP5, PP-19 Vityaz and Super-45 fit short-range movement better than long-lane duels. The Super-45 appears with Medic XP in the observed catalogue, but that progression gate can change. A medic kit should preserve storage for healing and recovery tools instead of filling every slot with drums and loose ammunition.

Shotguns and compact weapons are situational. Their value rises around interiors, alleys and stairwells and falls when the squad must cross exposed approaches. Pair the weapon with a route plan. If you cannot close distance safely, buying a close-range specialty does not solve the tactical problem.

## Support, marksman, and anti-vehicle choices

LMGs such as the M249 SAW and PKM are useful when a squad can feed ammunition and exploit suppression. Fire in controlled periods, change position after revealing the gun, and avoid spending the entire reserve on targets that are already behind hard cover. Marksman and sniper options should be selected for observation, lane control and priority targets, not only magnification.

Launchers and specialist anti-vehicle systems require target identification and a safe backblast or firing lane where applicable. Coordinate with vehicle crews and another anti-armor player. The catalogue records names such as MAAWS, RPG-7 and 9K333 Verba, but does not invent unverified penetration, lock range or damage values.

## Complete the loadout

1. Pick the role and likely engagement range.
2. Open the [ammunition catalogue](/en/items/ammo) and confirm the correct calibre.
3. Add compatible magazines and reserve storage before buying extra attachments.
4. Use the [attachments catalogue](/en/items/attachments) to select an optic for the actual route, not maximum zoom by default.
5. Reserve money and space for [gear and protection](/en/items/gear), medical supplies, and the squad task.
6. Test recoil, sight picture, reload flow and inventory placement in the current build.
7. Change one part at a time so you know what improved the kit.

## Budget and loss control

Persistent cash makes survival and replacement cost part of weapon choice. A modest rifle that returns from several useful fights can produce more value than an expensive specialist kit lost during an unsupported rush. Before spawning, ask whether the squad has logistics, whether the route needs armor or transport, and whether the objective pays for the risk.

Treat observed Alpha prices as comparison evidence, not a shopping list for launch. The safest budget rule is structural: primary weapon, working ammunition, storage, protection, role tool, and a reserve. If one purchase removes two of those pieces, the loadout is not complete.

## How to test a weapon fairly

Use the same range, optic, stance and ammunition type. Fire repeatable groups at short, medium and long distance; record controllability, visibility, reload interruption and how much storage the complete ammunition plan consumes. Then repeat after a patch. Do not turn one kill clip into a universal damage conclusion.

Separate confirmed identity from balance interpretation. The official Team17 press kit visibly identifies the M4 and Super-45 in gameplay HUDs. Other catalogue entries are labelled by their evidence tier and media status. Missing values remain missing until a source can verify them.

## FAQ

**What is the best weapon?** There is no confirmed universal winner; choose the complete kit that fits the route and squad task.

**Should I buy the most expensive rifle?** Only when the rest of the loadout, replacement budget, and team plan can support it.

**Are Alpha rankings still valid?** They are useful test leads, not permanent balance facts. Verify price, recoil, unlocks, and attachment support in the current build.
