## Quick Answer

The official WARDOGS system requirements start at **Windows 10**, an Intel Core i5-8600 or AMD Ryzen 5 3500, **16 GB RAM**, a GTX 1660 or RX 590, broadband internet and **50 GB** available storage. That minimum targets 1080p Low at **60fps** with upscaling. The recommended tier moves to Windows 11, a Core i7-12700K or Ryzen 7 5700X and an RTX 3070 or RX 6700 XT.

## Confirmed Facts

- WARDOGS is currently announced for 64-bit Windows PC through Steam.
- Minimum CPU: Intel Core i5-8600 or AMD Ryzen 5 3500.
- Minimum GPU: Nvidia GTX 1660 or AMD Radeon RX 590.
- Recommended CPU: Intel Core i7-12700K or AMD Ryzen 7 5700X.
- Recommended GPU: Nvidia RTX 3070 or AMD Radeon RX 6700 XT.
- Both tiers list **16 GB RAM**, broadband internet and **50 GB** available storage.
- Minimum target: 1080p Low at 60fps, upscaled.
- Recommended targets: 1440p Medium at 70fps+ native, or 4K Medium at 60fps+ upscaled.

## What Players Search For

Players searching `wardogs system requirements` or `wardogs sys req` usually need a simple yes-or-no hardware comparison. Compare the complete tier rather than matching only the graphics card. WARDOGS combines up to 100 players, vehicles, destruction and construction, which can make CPU load and frame-time stability important even when average GPU performance looks adequate.

Storage is another frequent source of confusion. Steam's **50 GB** figure is available disk space, not a promise that the network download will be exactly 50 GB. Installation, patches, shader caches and Windows itself may need additional headroom.

## How to Check Your PC

In Windows, open **Settings > System > About** for the processor, installed RAM and Windows version. Open **Task Manager > Performance > GPU** or the graphics driver's control panel to confirm the GPU model. In File Explorer, check the free space on the drive where Steam installs games.

Laptop component names can resemble desktop parts while operating at different power limits. Make sure WARDOGS uses the discrete GPU, connect the laptop to power, select an appropriate performance mode and watch temperatures during a busy match. A machine can meet the model name on paper while thermal or power limits reduce sustained performance.

## Performance Expectations

Test under real load. A quiet spawn, empty server or menu cannot represent a crowded Control Zone with vehicles, explosions and construction. Use a consistent location and compare the lowest sustained performance rather than one peak FPS number.

If resolution changes create a large improvement, the GPU is likely an important limit. If lowering resolution barely helps while large battles still drop, the CPU or current build may be the constraint. Follow the settings guide and change one variable at a time.

WARDOGS uses online multiplayer systems and the Steam listing identifies Easy Anti-Cheat. Broadband quality and local render performance are separate: rubber-banding or delayed server feedback can occur even when the frame counter is stable.

## FAQ

**Can I play WARDOGS with 8 GB RAM?**
It is below the official minimum. Both published tiers list 16 GB RAM.

**Is the GTX 1660 target native 1080p?**
No. Steam describes the minimum target as 1080p Low at 60fps with upscaling.

**Is an RTX 3070 enough for 4K?**
Steam lists an RTX 3070-class GPU for 4K Medium at 60fps+ with upscaling, or 1440p Medium at 70fps+ native. Actual results still depend on the full system and build.

**Do I need more than 50 GB free?**
Steam lists 50 GB available space. Extra room is sensible for patches, caches and temporary installation files.
