## Quick Answer

Use observed rewards in the cash/XP planner. Record an action, its duration and costs, then compare net cash and the relevant XP track. The tool estimates your inputs; it is not an official reward feed.

## Confirmed Facts

Update 0.1.2 changes exploit handling and revive validation. Old reward charts cannot establish a permanent current payout. Career XP, role XP and cash are different measurements, and the announced seasonal cash/XP reset affects late-season goals.

## Action Plan

1. Record cash, Career XP and one role XP before the task.
2. Complete an ordinary revive, transport, supply or objective task.
3. Record each change, elapsed time and equipment spending.
4. Enter the observed values in the planner; label any repeat or zone assumption.
5. Repeat after a patch before comparing routes.

## Worked example

Hypothetical arithmetic: 600 cash received in 10 minutes with 150 cash spent gives 450 net cash, or 45 per minute. These are example inputs, not WARDOGS rewards. If the next trip loses a vehicle, include replacement cost and waiting time instead of reporting only successful trips.

## Common mistake

A higher cash total is not automatically faster role progression. Do not multiply a first-action reward indefinitely or treat a selected Hot Zone/repeat assumption as proof of a server formula. Compare like-for-like tasks on the same build.
