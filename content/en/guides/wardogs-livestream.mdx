## Beta 02 schedule correction

The FPS Games Show began at **18:00 UTC on September 3**. A later official schedule notice moved the actual Beta 02 server opening to **19:00 UTC**; the closing time remains **September 6 at 08:00 UTC**. Older Steam copy and search snippets can still show 18:00 UTC as the beta start, so use 19:00 UTC for the revised server window and 18:00 UTC only for the broadcast.

The September 3 reveal is complete. Its actionable result is **Closed Beta 02**, running from **September 3 at 19:00 UTC to September 6 at 08:00 UTC**, plus the confirmed September 10 Steam Early Access milestone and the official $100K clip contest. Older pre-show speculation is no longer a current source.

## Closed Beta 02: current official status

Steam describes this as a closed scale test, not an open beta. The test covers server scaling, the browser, login queues, voice communication, Twitch Drops, and DLC entitlements. Capacity can still affect sessions. Free Request Access approvals arrive in waves; a pending request does not guarantee entry.

### What to do now

1. Open the official Steam page and sign in to the account that requested or purchased access.
2. Check the Library for the official WARDOGS entitlement and install control.
3. In the server browser, select **one region only** if the list appears empty.
4. If local voice fails, the developer suggests disabling voice chat once or opening the scoreboard and pressing **U twice**.
5. Treat Supporter Pack items as unavailable during the beta; the developer says they are intended to unlock in Early Access.
6. Record the exact error, UTC time, region, hardware, and driver before contacting support.

### After the beta

Steam Early Access remains scheduled for **September 10, 2026** on Windows PC. This guide does not invent an exact unlock hour, progress carryover, final balance, console release, or cross-play plan. Recheck Steam and official WARDOGS channels when the beta closes because a test entitlement, install state, or server list can change independently.

## What to Verify

Before relying on a stream, record who published it, which build was played, whether the player had authorized access, and when the session occurred. Then separate durable store facts from impressions. The Windows platform, core team structure, Control Zone concept, and persistent cash have official support; exact menus, controls, balance, and roster details may change.

For scheduled events, convert the time only after the official post names a time zone. Do not infer the beta's start hour from a creator's planned stream. A streamer can start before access, wait through maintenance, or cover recorded footage.

## VOD Safety

Avoid "watch to get a key" pages unless an official announcement explains the promotion and links the destination. Never supply a Steam Guard code, Discord token, browser cookie, or payment to unlock a replay or giveaway. A legitimate VOD does not require an executable codec or account verifier.

If an old broadcast contains test dates, pricing, platform claims, or access instructions, check them against the current Steam page before acting. Livestreams preserve a moment; they do not keep changing facts current.
