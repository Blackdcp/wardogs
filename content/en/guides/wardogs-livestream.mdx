## Official Broadcasts

An official broadcast should originate from or be directly linked by Team17, BULKHEAD, WARDOGS, or the Steam event page. Check the account name, destination domain, published date, time zone, and whether the post says live, scheduled, or replay. Reuploads and restreams can preserve footage while losing the original context.

This guide does not provide an unverified Twitch channel or claim that a particular replay remains online. Streaming pages can be renamed, deleted, made private, or replaced, so an archived title without a working source is not enough.

## What to Verify

Before relying on a stream, record who published it, which build was played, whether the player had authorized access, and when the session occurred. Then separate durable store facts from impressions. The Windows platform, core team structure, Control Zone concept, and persistent cash have official support; exact menus, controls, balance, and roster details may change.

For scheduled events, convert the time only after the official post names a time zone. Do not infer the beta's start hour from a creator's planned stream. A streamer can start before access, wait through maintenance, or cover recorded footage.

## VOD Safety

Avoid "watch to get a key" pages unless an official announcement explains the promotion and links the destination. Never supply a Steam Guard code, Discord token, browser cookie, or payment to unlock a replay or giveaway. A legitimate VOD does not require an executable codec or account verifier.

If an old broadcast contains test dates, pricing, platform claims, or access instructions, check them against the current Steam page before acting. Livestreams preserve a moment; they do not keep changing facts current.
