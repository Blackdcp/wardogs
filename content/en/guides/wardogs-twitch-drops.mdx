## Are WARDOGS Twitch Drops active now?

The official Closed Beta 02 announcement names **Twitch Drops** as one of the systems being tested at scale. That confirms the integration is part of the current test, but it does **not** by itself confirm a public reward name, required watch time, eligible channel list, regional availability, or deadline. Check **Twitch Inventory** and Twitch Drops & Rewards before watching; if no WARDOGS campaign and progress bar appear there, do not assume a stream title or chat command creates progress.

Beta 02's revised servers opened on September 3 at **19:00 UTC**, after the FPS Games Show at **18:00 UTC**, and are scheduled to close on **September 6 at 08:00 UTC**. Those server times are not automatically the start and end of a Drops campaign. Twitch can publish a separate window, and only the campaign panel can confirm it.

## What is confirmed

- BULKHEAD explicitly listed Twitch Drops among the Beta 02 systems under load testing.
- Steam Playtest or pre-purchase entitlement controls whether the game can be launched.
- Twitch Inventory controls visible Drop progress and claim state.
- The August Closed Beta campaign is historical and its terms do not roll forward automatically.
- No verified current source used here supplies a Beta 02 reward name, watch threshold, complete eligible-channel list, or delivery deadline.

That last point is a limit, not an invitation to guess. A creator can label a stream “Drops” before Twitch marks it eligible, and an old reward image can remain indexed after a campaign closes.

## Safe check before watching

1. Sign in directly at Twitch rather than through a chat link.
2. Open Drops & Rewards, then Inventory, and look for a live WARDOGS campaign.
3. Read the campaign dates, participating-channel rule, watch requirement, and account-link destination.
4. Open an eligible live channel from the campaign panel and verify that progress increases.
5. Claim the item in Twitch when the panel says the requirement is complete.
6. Keep a screenshot of the claimed state if delivery is delayed.

Do not install an extension, executable, browser profile, or “Drop activator.” A legitimate Twitch campaign does not require your Steam Guard code, Discord token, cookies, or remote control of the PC.

## Drops are not game access

Steam and Twitch perform different jobs. Steam holds the WARDOGS purchase, Playtest approval, library entry, installation, and launch permission. Twitch records viewing progress and campaign claims. Linking the two may be required to deliver a cosmetic or account reward, but it cannot turn a pending free Steam request into an invitation.

If WARDOGS is missing from the Steam Library, use the beta access or download guide. Reconnecting Twitch repeatedly will not create the missing game entitlement and can make it harder to identify which account originally claimed a reward.

## August campaign versus Beta 02

WARDOGS ran a Drops promotion around the August Closed Beta. That campaign ended, and its names, thresholds, channels, and timing belong only to that event. Beta 02 is current, and the developer is testing the Drops system, but a new public campaign must still be verified in Twitch Inventory or a fresh official announcement.

This distinction prevents two common errors: saying all WARDOGS Drops are over when the integration is currently being tested, and inventing a live campaign by copying the August rules. The accurate status is narrower: the Beta 02 integration is confirmed; public reward details remain unverified.

## Progress or reward missing

First confirm that the campaign panel exists and that the channel is eligible. Then check the correct Twitch account, visible progress, completed Claim action, and the exact linked Steam identity. Allow the published delivery period if one is shown. If a claimed reward still fails, preserve the Inventory record and contact the official support route with the campaign name and timestamps, never passwords or authentication codes.
