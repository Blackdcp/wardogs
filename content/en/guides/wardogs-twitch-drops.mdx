## How do WARDOGS Twitch Drops work?

During an active campaign, link the account specified by the official WARDOGS promotion, watch an eligible live channel for the required time, then claim the reward on Twitch. Drops can award campaign items, but they do **not** guarantee Closed Beta or Steam Playtest access.

## Quick Answer

The August WARDOGS Closed Beta and its Twitch Drops campaign have ended. No active new WARDOGS Drops campaign is confirmed as of August 24, 2026. Players who already claimed a reward should keep the correct Steam and Twitch accounts linked while checking delivery; a Drop never created beta access.

## Confirmed Facts

- WARDOGS announced Twitch Drops for the Closed Beta on August 21, 2026.
- That beta-linked campaign ended with the test on August 24, 2026.
- Players had to connect the relevant Steam and Twitch accounts before rewards could be delivered correctly.
- Progress came from eligible WARDOGS Beta livestreams during the campaign, not every channel showing the game.
- Completed rewards were claimed through Twitch Drops and Rewards during the active window.
- Twitch Drops do not grant access to the WARDOGS Playtest.

## What Players Search For

Searches for `wardogs twitch drops` usually hide two different questions: how to obtain the campaign reward and whether watching a stream unlocks the game. Keep those paths separate. Steam controls the playable entitlement; Twitch controls viewing progress and reward claims.

Players also search after a progress bar stops or a claimed reward does not appear. Most checks are account-state checks rather than reasons to relink repeatedly: correct accounts, eligible live channel, visible campaign progress, completed claim, and enough delivery time.

## How to Use This Guide

Use **Link Accounts** and **Watch and Claim** below as a record of the ended campaign and as a checklist only if WARDOGS officially announces a future one. For a previously claimed reward, do not disconnect accounts while delivery is pending, and never enter Steam Guard details into a page reached through a chat message or unofficial giveaway.

## Link Accounts

During the August campaign, viewers started from the account-link destination published by the official WARDOGS channel and connected the Steam and Twitch identities used for the promotion. If another campaign is announced, re-check its official account page and terms instead of assuming the archived route is still active.

Account linking is for reward delivery. It does not turn a free Steam Request Access application into an approved invitation. If WARDOGS Playtest is missing from your Steam library, use the [playtest guide](/guides/wardogs-playtest) rather than repeatedly changing the Twitch link.

## Watch and Claim

There is no active WARDOGS Drops campaign confirmed now. During the ended campaign, viewers opened WARDOGS inside Twitch Drops and Rewards, chose a participating live channel, and relied on the campaign progress page rather than estimated watch time.

Viewers who completed the August requirement had to press Claim in Twitch and keep Steam and Twitch connected until delivery. Individual reward names, watch thresholds, and delivery timing were temporary campaign fields and must not be reused for any future promotion without a new official announcement.

## Troubleshooting

Because the August campaign has ended, a progress bar that no longer moves is not a current troubleshooting issue and watching more cannot complete it. If a reward was already marked Claimed before the deadline, preserve the Twitch inventory record, verify the linked identities, and follow official support guidance.

If Twitch says Claimed but WARDOGS shows nothing, confirm the connected Steam identity and allow time for delivery. Save a screenshot of the claimed inventory and the account names before contacting official support. Do not pay a third party to “activate” a Drop and do not share cookies, passwords, QR codes, or Steam Guard codes.
