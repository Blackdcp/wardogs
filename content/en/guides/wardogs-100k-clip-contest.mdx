## Quick Answer

Entries are closed. The official deadline was October 8, 2026 at 23:59 BST (22:59 UTC), or October 9 at 06:59 in Beijing/Taipei and 07:59 in Tokyo. The published winner announcement is October 15 or shortly after; this is a planned announcement date, not a verified winners list.

## Confirmed Facts

The official terms govern entry eligibility and awards. The landing page describes a top 100 with 99 runners-up, while the formal terms list one $100,000 cash grand prize and 100 prizes of 100,000 units of in-game currency. Those two public descriptions differ. Do not turn either wording into a claim that every finalist receives real money; confirm the final allocation with the organizer.

## Action Plan

Keep your original clip, public post URL, completed-form record and the contact address used to enter. Check that inbox and the official contest site for the organizer’s announcement. A popular clip or a social reply is not a verified win. No winners have been independently verified by this guide on October 9.

Historical entry requirements: clips were limited to two minutes and posted on X, YouTube, TikTok or Instagram with #WARDOGS100K and @WARDOGS. Entrants also had to submit the official form before closing. The requirements below explain existing entries; they are not an invitation to submit late.

## Common mistake

Opening a still-visible form does not extend the published deadline. Only a dated organizer amendment can establish an extension. Do not replace or delete your submitted post merely to follow a rumor about judging; keep the entry and any correspondence available for verification.

## FAQ

No. The published entry deadline passed on October 8, 2026 at 23:59 BST. Check the organizer for any explicitly announced extension.
