## Storage

The checked requirement is **50 GB of available storage**. Available space is not the same measurement as compressed download size. Network transfers may be smaller, while unpacking, patches, temporary files, shader caches, recordings, and future updates can raise the space needed during normal use.

Leave additional headroom on the drive and avoid filling the volume to its limit. Steam may need temporary space to patch large archives even when the final installed footprint changes only slightly.
