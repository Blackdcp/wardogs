## Safe Download

There is no approved reason to use a third-party installer, repack, torrent, mobile APK, key activator, browser extension, or standalone launcher. WARDOGS is announced for Windows PC on Steam. A legitimate download becomes available in the library when the account owns the game or has an active test entitlement.

Search ads and direct messages can mimic publisher branding. Confirm the Steam App ID, developer BULKHEAD, and publisher Team17 before signing in or installing anything. Never enter Steam Guard details on a page reached through an unsolicited download offer.

## Install Steps

1. Install or update the official Steam client and sign into the account that requested access or purchased WARDOGS.
2. Open the official WARDOGS store page and confirm App 1867240. Complete the appropriate access or purchase flow.
3. Install the main **WARDOGS** product for Early Access. The old **WARDOGS Playtest** entry belonged to the completed Closed Beta and is not the current client.
4. Let Steam complete the download, installation, and Easy Anti-Cheat setup. Launch from the library rather than an unknown executable.
5. If files fail validation, use Steam's installed-files verification and official support options before reinstalling components from elsewhere.

## Storage

The checked requirement is **50 GB of available storage**. Available space is not the same measurement as compressed download size. Network transfers may be smaller, while unpacking, patches, temporary files, shader caches, recordings, and future updates can raise the space needed during normal use.

Leave additional headroom on the drive and avoid filling the volume to its limit. Steam may need temporary space to patch large archives even when the final installed footprint changes only slightly.

## Preload

The official August 20 schedule announcement confirmed that invited accounts could preload **WARDOGS Playtest** for the completed August Closed Beta. The separate Early Access pre-load later opened September 9 for the main WARDOGS product.

The beta server window has ended, so a missing Playtest entry is no longer a current access failure. Confirm account identity, purchase history, the main WARDOGS app, and Steam's current service status when installation or launch fails.

Do not solve an absent preload by obtaining copied game files. They may be outdated, modified, unsafe, or unusable without the correct Steam entitlement. The official client will deliver the build and updates when access is active.
