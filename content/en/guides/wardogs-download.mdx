## Beta 02 schedule correction

The FPS Games Show began at **18:00 UTC on September 3**. A later official schedule notice moved the actual Beta 02 server opening to **19:00 UTC**; the closing time remains **September 6 at 08:00 UTC**. Older Steam copy and search snippets can still show 18:00 UTC as the beta start, so use 19:00 UTC for the revised server window and 18:00 UTC only for the broadcast.

Install the current test only from the official Steam library entry granted to your account. **Closed Beta 02 runs from September 3 at 19:00 UTC to September 6 at 08:00 UTC**. Do not buy a third-party beta key or use a copied launcher if Steam has not granted access.

## Closed Beta 02: current official status

Steam describes this as a closed scale test, not an open beta. The test covers server scaling, the browser, login queues, voice communication, Twitch Drops, and DLC entitlements. Capacity can still affect sessions. Free Request Access approvals arrive in waves; a pending request does not guarantee entry.

### What to do now

1. Open the official Steam page and sign in to the account that requested or purchased access.

5. Treat Supporter Pack items as unavailable during the beta; the developer says they are intended to unlock in Early Access.
6. Record the exact error, UTC time, region, hardware, and driver before contacting support.

## Install Steps

1. Install or update the official Steam client and sign into the account that requested access or purchased WARDOGS.
2. Open the official WARDOGS store page and confirm App 1867240. Complete the appropriate access or purchase flow.
3. For Early Access, install the main WARDOGS product only when Steam enables it. The old **WARDOGS Playtest** entry belonged to the completed Closed Beta.

## Storage

The checked requirement is **50 GB of available storage**. Available space is not the same measurement as compressed download size. Network transfers may be smaller, while unpacking, patches, temporary files, shader caches, recordings, and future updates can raise the space needed during normal use.

Leave additional headroom on the drive and avoid filling the volume to its limit. Steam may need temporary space to patch large archives even when the final installed footprint changes only slightly.

## Preload

The official August 20 schedule announcement confirmed that invited accounts could preload **WARDOGS Playtest** for the completed August Closed Beta. It did not confirm a preload for the September 10 Early Access product.

The beta server window has ended, so a missing Playtest entry is no longer a current access failure. Confirm account identity and purchase history for Early Access ownership, then wait for a newly dated official install notice.

Do not solve an absent preload by obtaining copied game files. They may be outdated, modified, unsafe, or unusable without the correct Steam entitlement. The official client will deliver the build and updates when access is active.
