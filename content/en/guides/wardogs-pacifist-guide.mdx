## Quick answer

**Yes, you can contribute and earn cash without getting kills.** BULKHEAD's official Steam description explicitly rewards reviving squadmates, carrying friendly players to the zone and controlling the objective. It also describes supply flights and FOB building as ways to play. That is a support playstyle in a live PvP match, not a separate pacifist or PvE queue. You can still be shot, lose equipment and need to defend yourself.

## Three routes with first-party support

**Medic near the objective.** Stay close enough to a squad to reach downed teammates, but do not cross an exposed lane just because a revive icon appears. Help the team hold or retake the Control Zone, move with cover and choose a kit you can afford to replace. The official store confirms revives as a cash-rewarded action; it does not publish a permanent per-revive amount.

**Transport driver or pilot.** Ferry teammates toward the active zone rather than circling an empty spawn. A useful route has a pickup point, safe drop-off and return leg. The store explicitly lists transporting friendlies to the zone as a cash action and supply flights by helicopter as a possible role. Vehicle cost and any current reward must be checked in the live client; an old creator screenshot is not a current rate card.

**Objective and logistics support.** Help your team occupy the Control Zone, move supplies where builders ask for them, and support a defensible FOB when your squad needs one. The official page confirms objective control pays and presents FOB fortification as a play option. It does not establish that every supply handoff or construction action always pays the same amount. Judge a trip by useful team outcome and the reward actually shown after completion.

## A low-combat first-match loop

1. Read the current map and find the active Control Zone before buying a specialist loadout.
2. Choose one service: revives with your squad, short passenger transport, or requested logistics. Do not buy a costly vehicle just to test an unfamiliar route.
3. Take an affordable kit and keep enough cash for another deployment. Persistent cash means a lost loadout matters beyond one life.
4. Complete the job, watch the current reward display and note repair, fuel, ammunition or replacement costs. Compare **net** results, not one headline payout.
5. Repeat only while the route helps the objective. If nobody needs transport or supplies, switch to a different support task rather than farming empty actions.

## What “pacifist” does not mean here

WARDOGS has no official promise that a player can avoid all combat, deaths or losses. There is no official fixed income table for an always-profitable no-kill route. Match conditions, teammates and balance patches can change what works. Do not use XP-farming servers, staged interactions or economy exploits as a substitute for useful play; read the current official rules and patch feed before using a community server.

If your goal is simply to recover after a bad purchase, use the [money guide](/en/guides/wardogs-money-guide) for reserve budgeting and the [medic guide](/en/guides/wardogs-medic-revive-guide) or [cargo guide](/en/guides/wardogs-cargo-guide) for deeper role details. This page answers the narrower question: what can a player do *without chasing kills* while still helping the team?
