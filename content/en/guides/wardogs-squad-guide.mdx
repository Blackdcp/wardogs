## Quick Answer

Before joining, put every player in the same Steam region and voice channel, verify microphones, and decide how the group will reunite if the game separates it. Inside the match, use squad voice for plans and proximity voice for nearby coordination. WARDOGS has no fixed class picker: a squad creates roles through purchases, so choose a medic, transport or logistics player, and enough infantry for the current objective.

The exact invite menu, squad limit, queue behavior, and deployment options remain launch-sensitive. Steam's social tools are a practical fallback, but only the Early Access interface can confirm the final joining flow.

## Before the Queue

Agree on region, start time, preferred faction if selection is available, and who creates the group. Add every player on Steam and verify that profile privacy or family settings do not block invitations. Keep Discord or another external channel available until everyone is inside the same match and squad.

Do not promise one squad-size number. Community reports from tests described unusually large squads, but the official product information does not publish a final limit. Split a very large group into functional fire teams even if the interface allows everyone under one label.

## Voice Channels

Official descriptions emphasize local or proximity voice. Use it for passengers, nearby medics, builders, drivers, and strangers moving through the same area. Because local communication can be heard by nearby players in observed builds, bind push-to-talk and avoid announcing a hidden route beside an enemy position.

Use squad voice for information that must survive distance: objective choice, rally point, transport timing, enemy vehicle, supply request, retreat, and role changes. Keep calls short. A useful format is threat, direction, distance or landmark, and intended response.

## Flexible Roles, Not Locked Classes

WARDOGS follows a “what you buy is what you are” structure. Build the squad around tasks instead of permanent class names.

- **Infantry:** contests terrain, clears routes, and protects support players.
- **Medic:** carries enough recovery tools to preserve players and expensive kits.
- **Driver or pilot:** moves the squad and maintains a return route.
- **Logistics:** asks what resource is needed and completes repeat deliveries.
- **Builder:** places only structures the position can supply and defend.
- **Recon:** spots routes and threats, then relocates before becoming predictable.

One player may cover two light roles, but avoid making the only medic the only pilot and the only squad leader. A single loss should not remove every recovery option.

## Rally and Transport Plan

Beta observations did not provide ordinary spawn-on-squadmate behavior. Assume a death costs travel time until the current deployment screen proves otherwise. Pick a rally point near safe transport, a fallback behind the objective, and a rule for when the squad stops waiting for one delayed player.

Drivers and pilots should announce departure and destination. Passengers should be equipped and ready before the vehicle arrives. On landing or dismount, spread into cover instead of forming one explosive target. Keep the vehicle oriented toward an exit or decide explicitly that it is joining the fight.

## Revives and Spacing

Stay close enough for trades and revives but far enough that one grenade, mortar round, or vehicle weapon cannot remove the whole squad. Suppress or identify the threat before reviving. One player recovers the casualty while others watch the firing angle and possible third team.

Do not give up immediately when a medic is safe and nearby. Do not force a revive in open terrain only to lose another kit. The squad leader should call when recovery is possible and when the group must reset.

## Objective and Economy

The team needs living presence in the Control Zone even when specialists operate outside it. Build every support task around objective timing: transport delivers bodies, logistics keeps a useful FOB supplied, recon watches a real approach, and a medic preserves the push. A squad that farms far from scoring may earn individual cash while losing the match.

Share economic risk. One player can field the expensive vehicle while others bring repairs, anti-air awareness, medical support, or escort. Avoid five players independently buying the same specialist answer. The [money guide](/guides/wardogs-money-guide) explains how team actions can support the wallet.

## Playing Solo

Solo players can use proximity voice at the start of a match to find an organized group. Ask which squad is communicating, state a useful role, and follow the existing plan before trying to lead strangers. Mute abuse, keep personal information private, and use official community channels for longer-term groups.

## Launch-Day Checks

Verify the invite route, squad limit, faction joining, server browser or matchmaking behavior, voice defaults, mute controls, radio bindings, and deployment options on September 10. Label beta observations by date and do not turn a normal Steam feature into a claimed WARDOGS guarantee.

## Confirmed Facts

- WARDOGS is designed around large team battles and combined roles.
- Beta players used Steam and in-game grouping, plus local proximity communication.
- Early Access invitation flow, group limits, and persistence between matches still require live verification.

## What Players Search For

Players searching for squads want to join friends, find a team as a solo player, understand voice channels, and regroup after a separated spawn. Those problems are solved by a shared external channel, a precise meeting point, short local calls, and flexible roles.

## How to Use This Guide

Complete the pre-server checklist with friends, use the first-minute script when joining alone, and apply the small-squad plan before taking a vehicle. Recheck the launch section whenever grouping behavior changes.

## FAQ

**Can I use Steam to organize friends?** It was used during testing, but the final invite sequence needs live verification.

**When should I use proximity voice?** Use it for players, vehicles, treatment, and immediate threats near your position.

**Do roles have to stay fixed?** No. Driver, Medic, logistics, and objective duties should change with the situation.
