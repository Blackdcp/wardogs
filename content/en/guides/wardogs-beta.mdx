## Quick Answer

The WARDOGS Closed Beta is active for August 21-23, 2026 on Steam. Pre-orders qualify for guaranteed access, Steam Request Access remains a selection chance, the first invite wave has been sent, and invited accounts can preload the separate **WARDOGS Playtest** library entry.

## Confirmed Facts

- The WARDOGS Closed Beta date range is August 21-23, 2026.
- Pre-order customers are promised guaranteed Closed Beta access under Steam's published terms.
- Request Access applicants may be selected, but that route is not guaranteed.
- The first invite wave was sent and WARDOGS Playtest became available for preload before the beta.
- Progress carryover, complete server-region coverage, and any extension beyond August 23 are not confirmed.

## What Players Search For

Players searching `wardogs beta` usually want to know whether they can still get in, whether a key is required, and whether beta progress will matter later. The safest answer is to treat beta as a temporary Steam test unless official posts say otherwise.

## How to Use This Guide

Use **Dates** to separate beta from Early Access. Use **Eligibility** before buying or waiting on a request. Use **Build Expectations** and **Progress** to set realistic expectations for a pre-release weekend.

## Dates

The confirmed public window is August 21-23. The August 20 schedule announcement also confirms that the first wave of invitations was sent and that additional free Steam-request accounts may be added cautiously while server scaling is evaluated. That is not a promise that every pending request will be approved.

Do not turn community queue screenshots, regional delays, or one player's login state into a universal service claim. Those reports can reveal demand, but only a new official announcement can confirm maintenance, a schedule change, or a beta extension.

The test is separate from the September 10, 2026 Early Access launch. A beta entitlement permits participation in this test window; it is not a statement that Early Access has launched early or that the complete game is available.

## Eligibility

There are two clearly different access routes. A pre-order carries guaranteed Closed Beta entry according to the announced terms. The free Request Access button on Steam submits an application and may result in an invitation, but it is not guaranteed. In either case, access should appear through the Steam account used for the request or purchase.

Discord membership, old Alpha roles, creator giveaways, and legacy FirstLook registration should not be treated as current proof of beta eligibility. If an invitation arrives, confirm that the entitlement appears in Steam rather than trusting a message that asks you to sign in through an unfamiliar page.

## Build Expectations

This is a testing build of an in-development large-scale FPS. Players should expect balance adjustments, technical faults, incomplete polish, and possible differences from trailers or creator footage. Server capacity, available regions, map rotation, anti-cheat behavior, and the exact feature set for the weekend have not been fully published.

Preload is confirmed for invited accounts under the separate **WARDOGS Playtest** name in Steam. Update the Steam client, open the Library rather than relying only on the main store page, and search for that exact entry. Avoid any third-party installer offered as a beta package; the legitimate build and updates come through Steam.

## Progress

No official promise says that beta cash, XP, unlocks, settings, or statistics will persist into Early Access. The practical assumption is that test data may be reset. Spend time learning the Control Zone flow, team roles, logistics, and performance settings rather than optimizing a permanent progression plan around an unconfirmed carryover.

If the Playtest entry is missing after a qualifying pre-order, confirm that the purchase and library use the same Steam account, restart the client, remove active library filters, and check official announcements. A pending free request is not an entitlement. High demand or a visible queue does not mean a local reinstall will create access.
