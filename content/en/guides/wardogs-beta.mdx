## Quick Answer

The WARDOGS Closed Beta is scheduled for August 21-23, 2026 on Steam. Request Access is the free route but only offers a chance of selection; pre-ordering is the paid route that guarantees access under the published terms. The start time and preload remain unconfirmed.

## Confirmed Facts

- The WARDOGS Closed Beta date range is August 21-23, 2026.
- Pre-order customers are promised guaranteed Closed Beta access under Steam's published terms.
- Request Access applicants may be selected, but that route is not guaranteed.
- Progress carryover, preload, server regions, and exact start/end times are not confirmed.

## How to Use This Guide

Use **Dates** to separate beta from Early Access. Use **Eligibility** before buying or waiting on a request. Use **Build Expectations** and **Progress** to set realistic expectations for a pre-release weekend.

## Dates

The confirmed information is the date range, not an hour-by-hour timetable. BULKHEAD has not published a universal opening time, closing time, or time zone in the material checked for this guide. Do not convert an unofficial countdown into a claimed launch schedule. Recheck the Steam store and official WARDOGS posts close to August 21, especially if you are planning from outside Europe.

The test is separate from the September 10, 2026 Early Access launch. A beta entitlement permits participation in this test window; it is not a statement that Early Access has launched early or that the complete game is available.

## Eligibility

There are two clearly different access routes. A pre-order carries guaranteed Closed Beta entry according to the announced terms. The free Request Access button on Steam submits an application and may result in an invitation, but it is not guaranteed. In either case, access should appear through the Steam account used for the request or purchase.

Discord membership, old Alpha roles, creator giveaways, and legacy FirstLook registration should not be treated as current proof of beta eligibility. If an invitation arrives, confirm that the entitlement appears in Steam rather than trusting a message that asks you to sign in through an unfamiliar page.

## Build Expectations

This is a testing build of an in-development large-scale FPS. Players should expect balance adjustments, technical faults, incomplete polish, and possible differences from trailers or creator footage. Server capacity, available regions, map rotation, anti-cheat behavior, and the exact feature set for the weekend have not been fully published.

Preload is also unconfirmed. Keep enough storage available, update the Steam client, and avoid downloading any third-party installer offered as an early beta package. The legitimate install control should come from the Steam library or official store page.

## Progress

No official promise says that beta cash, XP, unlocks, settings, or statistics will persist into Early Access. The practical assumption is that test data may be reset. Spend time learning the Control Zone flow, team roles, logistics, and performance settings rather than optimizing a permanent progression plan around an unconfirmed carryover.

When the test begins, verify live status through Steam announcements before diagnosing a local problem. If servers are limited or maintenance occurs, community reports can be useful signals, but only an official post can define the expected service window.
