## Quick Answer

Use a cargo-capable vehicle, open its logistics interface, buy the crate or pallet the destination needs, place it in a compatible bed slot, and deliver it to an active FOB or contested route. Closed Beta footage used **B** to open the vehicle logistics screen, **G** to drop cargo, and **F** to unload at a valid destination. These are beta controls, so read the live prompts before relying on them.

## Choose the Vehicle for the Load

The recorded logistics walkthrough separated passenger vehicles from cargo beds. The Bobcat carried people but was not shown carrying cargo. The buggy also lacked demonstrated container space. Kodiak variants and Humvees could take smaller containers, while the **Ural** was the practical choice for full supply pallets. Armed variants may add protection, but a weapon does not increase bed capacity.

Do not choose by speed alone. A small vehicle may fit a quick crate and use narrow routes, while a Ural carries a larger investment that needs escort, turning room, and a safe unloading area. Exact slot counts and prices came from the August test and may change at Early Access.

## Open the Logistics Screen

In the creator footage, the driver entered the vehicle and pressed **B** to open the logistics screen. That interface separated the vehicle's normal inventory from bed or logistics slots. Internal inventory is useful for tools and smaller equipment; bed slots accept the supported crates and pallets.

Check the current keybind and the vehicle's on-screen prompt. The same key may have another role in infantry, building, or aircraft contexts. If the logistics screen does not appear, confirm that the vehicle actually supports cargo and that the player is using the correct seat or vendor area.

## Crates and Pallets

Small and large crates trade bed space for general storage. Armored crate variants were shown behind progression gates and served the same basic storage purpose with added protection. Do not publish one capacity or unlock level as permanent until the Early Access build is checked.

Pallets are purpose-built supply loads. The test menu showed **build, ammo, fuel, and mechanical** pallets. Build supplies feed construction; ammo supports weapons and emplacements; fuel supports vehicles or fuel-dependent structures; mechanical supplies support repair or selected systems. Ask the receiving FOB which resource is short before paying for the run.

## Loading the Vehicle

Build the manifest before leaving base. Count the vehicle, cargo, fuel or risk, and the cost of losing the entire bed. Fill the largest required object first, then use remaining space for smaller crates. Avoid carrying four resource types when the destination requested only one; mixed loads look flexible but often waste expensive capacity.

Keep the driver's combat kit light enough to survive an ambush and operate the vehicle. A passenger or escort can carry medical tools, anti-vehicle equipment, or construction tools. The cargo runner should not be the only person capable of defending or unloading the delivery.

## Route and Escort Planning

Mark the destination, primary road, alternate route, and turnaround point before departure. Roads are fast but predictable. Mud, slopes, trees, damaged bridges, and narrow compounds can stop a heavy truck before enemy fire does. The Ural footage showed that terrain and traction matter, so avoid treating the shortest line as the safest route.

Use a scout or escort when the load is expensive. Vary repeated runs after the enemy identifies the route. Announce the arrival so builders clear the unloading area and defenders watch the final approach. Parking a truck inside a blocked enclosure can turn a successful delivery into a trapped asset.

## Drop and Unload

The Closed Beta walkthrough used **G** to drop a pallet from the vehicle. Dropping supplies in an irrelevant area produced little or no useful reward. The creator then held **F** at a valid FOB to transfer the cargo into the base's usable supply pool. The live interface should confirm whether the destination accepts that resource.

Deliveries near an active, useful FOB or contested area produced better recorded value than dumping cargo far from demand. Treat the exact payouts shown in one match as examples, not a launch income table. The durable lesson is to finish the logistics loop: requested resource, compatible vehicle, safe route, valid unloading point, and a return plan.

## Small Crates and Manual Delivery

Smaller crates can sometimes be moved from a vehicle into a player's backpack and carried to the FOB computer or receiving interface. This is useful when the truck cannot reach the final position, but it exposes the player and consumes inventory space. Secure the final meters before unloading by hand.

## Common Cargo Mistakes

- Buying a pallet for a vehicle that only accepts small containers.
- Delivering the wrong resource to a fully stocked FOB.
- Dropping cargo outside the valid transfer area and assuming the job is complete.
- Blocking the only vehicle entrance while unloading.
- Repeating one ambushed road because it was fastest in the previous match.
- Copying beta capacity, price, payout, or keybind values as permanent Early Access rules.

## FAQ

**Does the Ural carry pallets?** It does in the checked beta walkthrough, but current capacity must be verified live.

**Why will the pallet not unload?** Check selection, distance, space, destination permissions, and whether the resource type matches the receiver.

**Are B, G, and F final?** No. They are recorded beta controls and can change.
