WARDOGS is scheduled to release in Steam Early Access on September 10, 2026 for Windows PC. No exact unlock hour or time zone has been announced, and the full version does not yet have a fixed release date.

## Confirmed Date

September 10, 2026 is the confirmed Early Access date. This is the date for the paid work-in-progress release, not the August Closed Beta and not a claim that development is complete. The beta runs August 21-23 for eligible accounts and is a separate testing window.

Dates can still be updated on digital stores, so the Steam listing remains the final place to check immediately before launch. Avoid treating third-party countdowns as authoritative, especially when they assume midnight UTC or local midnight without an official unlock schedule.

## Platform

The announced launch platform is Windows PC through Steam. Steam App 1867240 is the official store listing. No PlayStation 5, Xbox, or other console release date is part of the current announcement, and cross-play has not been promised.

The platform scope also matters for access: a Steam beta or pre-order entitlement is not a console license. Players should use the hardware requirements and platform details shown on Steam when deciding whether the Windows build is appropriate for them.

## Launch Time

The exact unlock time remains unconfirmed. A date alone does not establish whether Steam will unlock the game at midnight, at a publisher-selected global hour, or in regional waves. Until Team17, BULKHEAD, WARDOGS, or Steam posts a time, any clock shown elsewhere is an estimate.

Preload has not been confirmed either. Keep the Steam client updated and enough storage available, but do not download packages advertised as an early unlock. The legitimate install button will appear through Steam when the account and release state permit it.

## Full Release

Steam's Early Access statement estimates that WARDOGS may remain in Early Access for around one to two years. That is a development estimate, not a full-release appointment. The duration may change as the team expands content, improves systems, refines balance, and responds to community feedback.

The planned full version is broader and more polished, with additional maps, weapons, vehicles, progression, seasonal systems, objective variety, and continued balance, performance, and stability work. Those plans describe direction rather than guaranteed delivery dates. Use the September date only for the Windows Steam Early Access launch.
