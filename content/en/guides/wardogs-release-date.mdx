## WARDOGS release status

WARDOGS **launched in Steam Early Access on September 10, 2026** for Windows PC. It is available to buy and play through Steam now; the old Playtest and Beta 02 entries are not the release build.

The official launch trailer also says WARDOGS is **coming to consoles in 2028**. It does not name individual console platforms, release dates, cross-play, cross-progression, or features, so those details remain unconfirmed. The full 1.0 release has no fixed date.

## Platform

The announced launch platform is Windows PC through Steam. Steam App 1867240 is the official store listing. No PlayStation 5, Xbox, or other console release date is part of the current announcement, and cross-play has not been promised.

## Full Release

Steam's Early Access statement estimates that WARDOGS may remain in Early Access for around one to two years. That is a development estimate, not a full-release appointment. The duration may change as the team expands content, improves systems, refines balance, and responds to community feedback.
