## Season 2 launch-day checklist: before, during and after

Use **October 15** as the announced Season 2 date, not a confirmed reopening hour. The October 8 devlog preview did not supply an exact maintenance schedule. Work through the stage that matches your actual state:

| Stage | Do this | Pass condition / fallback |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before the update | Save dated screenshots of Cash, Gold, cosmetics and role/Career XP separately; write down kit and audio/key settings; reserve Steam staging space; agree a backup server with friends | You can compare individual balances with the [reset policy](/en/guides/wardogs-progression-wipes-guide), without assuming every value survives |
| Download/install | Use the main WARDOGS app, let both download and disk installation finish, then restart the client and record its build | If Steam reports a file error, follow that error and verify files; repeated reinstalling is not a cure for maintenance |
| Servers reopening | Read the official notice and the exact browser/join result | Maintenance/version mismatch: wait for a compatible server; progressing full-server queue: wait or switch together; admission restriction: choose an eligible server |
| First match | Confirm faction/squad, one voice exchange, bindings, available kit and ammunition; buy a small complete kit and perform one short task | Record a normal spawn, working controls and one completed task before buying a specialist vehicle |
| Something looks missing or broken | Compare the named Cash/XP/Gold/cosmetic item to the stated policy; keep before/after screens and error text | Stop repeated exchanges or purchases. Use [official reporting/support](/en/guides/wardogs-report-player) with the exact discrepancy |

Keep one log: **time/timezone — client build — server/region — stage — expected result — actual result — screenshot — next action**. For stutter, keep the same scene/settings and change one variable before retesting; a full server is not an FPS problem. If friends cannot join, use the [solo plan](/en/guides/wardogs-solo-guide) and preserve the agreed replacement reserve.

## Quick Answer

Existing players should prepare for the October 15 season transition: save current settings and balances, install the main game's update, verify server and squad, then complete one affordable task. New players also complete the purchase checks. Keep a replacement reserve.

## September 10: What Was Confirmed

WARDOGS launched on Windows PC through Steam Early Access on September 10, 2026. The published game scope includes up to 100 players, three factions, cash, vehicles, construction, destruction, logistics and a changing Control Zone. This is a completed release milestone; October players need current patch and server notices, not an Early Access unlock countdown.

## Purchase and Account Checklist

**For new buyers:** check the Steam edition, live regional checkout amount, refund terms and the account that will own the main game. Confirm Steam Guard, email access and a working payment method. The [price guide](/en/guides/wardogs-price) explains editions; the checkout sets the charged amount.

**Already own WARDOGS:** update the main library app for Season 2; this checklist does not require buying it again. The old Playtest entitlement is separate from the purchased game, and an old beta key is not an upgrade requirement.

## Download, Storage, Drivers, and Settings

The September 9 preload is historical. For the Season 2 patch, read the download and installation space Steam actually requests and leave room to unpack it. Update the main game; do not switch to an old test branch merely because it remains listed.

Keep screenshots of working graphics, controls and audio. Keep stable drivers unchanged unless an identified issue or published requirement calls for an update. If you change a driver, record old/new versions, change one component and retest the same scene before changing Windows, audio or graphics settings. Avoid a stack of untested changes immediately before joining. Use [requirements](/en/guides/wardogs-system-requirements), [settings](/en/guides/wardogs-best-settings) and [controls](/en/guides/wardogs-controls) for the failed check.

## Squad and Communication Checklist

Agree region, exact server, faction, squad and backup server before queueing. Test microphone and push-to-talk with a teammate, and choose a fallback text or voice channel. Assign jobs to the current need: observation, transport, medicine, supply and objective infantry.

After the patch, verify every friend reached the same server, faction and squad separately. If one layer is full or restricted, change that layer together instead of treating it as an outage. Use the [squad checklist](/en/guides/wardogs-squad-guide) for mixed levels and listen-only members.

## First-Match Loadout

Begin with a controllable weapon, compatible magazines, loose ammunition, basic healing, and only the storage needed for that job. Keep a cash reserve for the second life. Do not buy the most expensive armor, specialist launcher, backpack, and vehicle before learning the live prices and objective flow.

Open the map, identify the Control Zone, find organized transport, and stay close enough to trade or revive. A first match should teach the vendor, ammunition loop, deployment, travel route, objective, and support rewards. Use the [beginner guide](/en/guides/wardogs-beginner-guide), [ammo guide](/en/guides/wardogs-ammo-reload-guide), and [money guide](/en/guides/wardogs-money-guide) as the pre-deployment sequence.

## Launch-Day Verification

For an existing owner preparing for Season 2, use this order:

1. Read the October 15 transition notice and record any stated timezone; do not infer a reopening hour.
2. Confirm the main WARDOGS app is in your library. Only new buyers need the account/checkout section below.
3. Finish the game update and leave SSD space for installation. Keep a stable driver baseline; if a documented issue requires a change, change one component and test before changing another.
4. Save current working graphics, bindings and audio settings, plus separate Cash/XP/Gold/cosmetic screenshots.
5. Confirm region, exact server, faction, squad and backup communication with friends.
6. Record the new build and complete one inexpensive task before committing to specialist equipment.

## Confirmed vs Unconfirmed

**Confirmed now:** Early Access launched September 10 and preload began September 9. Season 2 is announced for October 15. BULKHEAD's stated seasonal policy resets cash and XP, converts remaining cash into Gold, and preserves Gold and cosmetics; see the [reset matrix](/en/guides/wardogs-progression-wipes-guide).

**Not confirmed in the reviewed Season 2 notice:** exact maintenance and reopening times, final download size, final weapon balance/prices, the boundary conversion rate and item-specific paid-unlock treatment. After transition, compare actual balances with the announced policy and keep any discrepancy in a support record. Do not turn an unknown operational detail into a claim that all carryover rules are unknown.
