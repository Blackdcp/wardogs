## Purchase and Account Checklist

Confirm which Steam edition is in the cart, the live regional price, refund terms shown by Steam, and whether the account can install the game. Use the maintained [price guide](/en/guides/wardogs-price) for edition context, but let the checkout page be authoritative for the amount charged.

Make sure Steam Guard, payment, e-mail access, and sufficient account storage are ready. Avoid buying third-party keys advertised as beta access. The August Closed Beta has ended, and an old test entitlement is not proof of a new access window.

## Download, Storage, Drivers, and Settings

Keep enough free SSD space for the installed game plus patching overhead. Pre-load went live on September 9, but current install and patch size must still be read from your own Steam client. Let Steam verify or replace the old build when the release branch appears. If the client offers a separate test branch, read its label before selecting it.

Update the GPU driver, Windows patches, Steam client, and audio drivers before the first session or a major patch. Save screenshots of working graphics and control settings because updates can reset values. Review the [system requirements](/en/guides/wardogs-system-requirements), [settings guide](/en/guides/wardogs-best-settings), and [controls guide](/en/guides/wardogs-controls) before troubleshooting under server load.

## Squad and Communication Checklist

Decide whether the group will use Steam voice, Discord, squad radio, or proximity voice for different tasks. Verify microphones and push-to-talk before joining a full server. Assign flexible opening jobs rather than permanent classes: one driver or pilot, one medic, one logistics player, and enough infantry to contest the active zone.

Steam invitations and in-game grouping details may change at launch. Agree on region, start time, faction selection if available, and a fallback meeting method. The [squad guide](/en/guides/wardogs-squad-guide) records what is official and what came from the beta.

## First-Match Loadout

Begin with a controllable weapon, compatible magazines, loose ammunition, basic healing, and only the storage needed for that job. Keep a cash reserve for the second life. Do not buy the most expensive armor, specialist launcher, backpack, and vehicle before learning the live prices and objective flow.

Open the map, identify the Control Zone, find organized transport, and stay close enough to trade or revive. A first match should teach the vendor, ammunition loop, deployment, travel route, objective, and support rewards. Use the [beginner guide](/en/guides/wardogs-beginner-guide), [ammo guide](/en/guides/wardogs-ammo-reload-guide), and [money guide](/en/guides/wardogs-money-guide) as the pre-deployment sequence.

## Launch-Day Verification

After the first live session, recheck every beta-sensitive statement: keybinds, vehicle cargo capacity, payouts, unlock levels, item prices, spawn rules, tower timing, Oil Rig costs, and ammunition behavior. Record the build and date with every correction. Early Access documentation should follow the game, not force the game to match an old video.

## Confirmed vs Unconfirmed

**Confirmed:** Closed Beta 02; the official $100K clip contest; the September 10 Steam Early Access date; Windows PC focus; and the core combined-arms, cash, logistics, construction, and objective systems.

**Not confirmed:** another open beta; Beta progress carryover; permanent beta prices; individual PS5 or Xbox dates; cross-play; cross-progression; and the final 1.0 schedule. Any of these changes only when a new first-party announcement or the live build provides evidence.

## Confirmed Facts
