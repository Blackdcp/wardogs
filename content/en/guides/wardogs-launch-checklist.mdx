## September 2 Firing Range Test Update

WARDOGS has announced a limited **Firing Range and backend/login-queue test for September 2, 2026 at 17:00 UTC**. This is **not an open beta**. The developers said they may run a handful of servers for the evening while testing backend features ahead of The FPS Games Show, but server availability is not promised.

The start converts to **18:00 BST**, **19:00 CEST**, **20:00 MSK**, **14:00 BRT**, **September 3 at 01:00 China Standard Time**, and **September 3 at 02:00 JST**. Use the official Steam client and existing WARDOGS Playtest entitlement if the test appears for your account; do not download a separate installer or buy a key.

This September 1 update supersedes older statements below saying that no later test had been announced. The August Closed Beta history remains useful, but it does not turn the September 2 backend test into a guaranteed public session.

## Quick Answer

WARDOGS is scheduled to enter Steam Early Access on **September 10, 2026** for Windows PC. Before then, watch the official **September 3** appearance at The FPS Games Show for the promised major announcement. Confirmed facts stop there: an open beta, an earlier unlock, a preload hour, console versions, and progress carryover are **not confirmed**.

Use the remaining time to check the Steam edition, free storage, drivers, input bindings, microphone, squad plan, and a low-risk first loadout. Do not spend launch week solving problems that can be identified before the servers open.

## September 3: Watch the Announcement, Not the Rumor Cycle

The official Steam notice directs players to The FPS Games Show on Twitch on September 3 at 18:00 UTC for a major announcement. Record the facts announced on stream: access window, preload instructions, launch time, build changes, server regions, progression rules, and any rewards. Treat clips, creator predictions, and social posts as commentary until Steam, BULKHEAD, Team17, or a verified WARDOGS channel repeats the information.

Creators have speculated about another beta or stress test before September 10. That scenario may be plausible, but it is **not confirmed**. Do not promise players free access, preserved progression, or a September 3 release. Our [livestream guide](/guides/wardogs-livestream) tracks the broadcast and separates the announcement from predictions.

## September 10: What Is Confirmed

The Steam store lists September 10, 2026 as the Early Access date. The announced product is a Windows PC game on Steam. The store describes up to 100 players, three factions, a persistent cash economy, vehicles, construction, destruction, logistics, and a randomized Control Zone. Early Access means systems, balance, content, performance, prices inside the game, and progression pacing can change after launch.

No exact unlock hour is currently published. Time-zone countdown pages should therefore show the date and an “hour pending” status instead of inventing a midnight launch. Check the store page and official announcements again after September 3.

## Purchase and Account Checklist

Confirm which Steam edition is in the cart, the live regional price, refund terms shown by Steam, and whether the account can install the game. Use the maintained [price guide](/guides/wardogs-price) for edition context, but let the checkout page be authoritative for the amount charged.

Make sure Steam Guard, payment, e-mail access, and sufficient account storage are ready. Avoid buying third-party keys advertised as beta access. The August Closed Beta has ended, and an old test entitlement is not proof of a new access window.

## Download, Storage, Drivers, and Settings

Keep enough free SSD space for the installed game plus patching overhead. A preload size and time are not confirmed, so do not plan around one recorded beta download. Let Steam verify or replace the old build when the release branch appears. If the client offers a separate test branch, read its label before selecting it.

Update the GPU driver, Windows patches, Steam client, and audio drivers before launch day. Save screenshots of working graphics and control settings if you played the beta, but expect some values to reset. Review the [system requirements](/guides/wardogs-system-requirements), [settings guide](/guides/wardogs-best-settings), and [controls guide](/guides/wardogs-controls) before troubleshooting under server load.

## Squad and Communication Checklist

Decide whether the group will use Steam voice, Discord, squad radio, or proximity voice for different tasks. Verify microphones and push-to-talk before joining a full server. Assign flexible opening jobs rather than permanent classes: one driver or pilot, one medic, one logistics player, and enough infantry to contest the active zone.

Steam invitations and in-game grouping details may change at launch. Agree on region, start time, faction selection if available, and a fallback meeting method. The [squad guide](/guides/wardogs-squad-guide) records what is official and what came from the beta.

## First-Match Loadout

Begin with a controllable weapon, compatible magazines, loose ammunition, basic healing, and only the storage needed for that job. Keep a cash reserve for the second life. Do not buy the most expensive armor, specialist launcher, backpack, and vehicle before learning the live prices and objective flow.

Open the map, identify the Control Zone, find organized transport, and stay close enough to trade or revive. A first match should teach the vendor, ammunition loop, deployment, travel route, objective, and support rewards. Use the [beginner guide](/guides/wardogs-beginner-guide), [ammo guide](/guides/wardogs-ammo-reload-guide), and [money guide](/guides/wardogs-money-guide) as the pre-deployment sequence.

## Launch-Day Verification

After the first live session, recheck every beta-sensitive statement: keybinds, vehicle cargo capacity, payouts, unlock levels, item prices, spawn rules, tower timing, Oil Rig costs, and ammunition behavior. Record the build and date with every correction. Early Access documentation should follow the game, not force the game to match an old video.

## Confirmed vs Unconfirmed

**Confirmed:** September 3 major announcement stream; September 10 Steam Early Access date; Windows PC focus; the core combined-arms, cash, logistics, construction, and objective systems.

**Not confirmed:** another open beta; exact unlock or preload time; progress carryover; final server regions; permanent beta prices; PS5 or Xbox dates; final 1.0 schedule. Any of these may change when the September 3 announcement arrives.
