## Patch 0.11: deployed September 14

WARDOGS is live in Steam Early Access. The maintenance window ran on **September 14 at 08:00 UTC for approximately one hour** and **Patch 0.11 is now deployed**. No further maintenance window has been announced.

### Shipped in Patch 0.11

- Separate **Community** and **Official** server browsers.
- More reliable Community filters, text search, persistent server IDs, and Show Empty/Full controls.
- A corrected Host link plus normalized server ordering and naming: names must be alphabetical and may not contain more than one consecutive space.
- Fixes for multiple cash exploits and more GPU crash paths.
- Increased server capacity in Asia.
- A 5% End of Match cash bonus on Community servers for the week following the patch.

### Announced for later, not included yet

Community favourites, tiered priority queues, a Message of the Day prompt, improved warmup activities, seeding-specific AFK controls, and team-switching controls are future work. They are not Patch 0.11 features until a later note says they shipped.

### Enforcement that shipped with the patch

BULKHEAD's policy states that abusing cash exploits or bugs leads to a timed ban with **cash and XP reset to 0**, that creating content which tutorialises exploits is treated like advertising cheats and draws a **permanent ban**, and that accounts recorded inside XP-farming servers for more than a few minutes can be **permanently banned**. Report an exploit to the developer; do not publish a tutorial for it.

Restart Steam if the client has not updated, then check the official notice before retrying. Finish a match before any future maintenance window.

## What counts as an official patch note

A tracked release needs a first-party Steam announcement or a directly attributable BULKHEAD, Team17, or WARDOGS notice. Each entry should include the publication date, build or version identifier when supplied, affected systems, and a source link.

Server maintenance and emergency notices are recorded separately from client patches. A server restart may change availability without downloading a new build; a Steam download may also contain packaging changes that are not a documented balance patch.

## Current launch watchlist

- Steam release state and the first installable Early Access branch.

- Crash, anti-cheat, RAM, VRAM, upscaling, shadow, and Reflex changes.
- Weapon, armor, ammunition, vehicle, cash, progression, and unlock adjustments.
- Controller, HOTAS, keybind, UI, accessibility, and localization corrections.

These are monitoring categories, not claims that a change has occurred.

## Confirmed Facts

WARDOGS launched in Early Access on September 10. The official Season 1 changelog, Launch Stability Hotfix #1, and Patch 0.11 are now live, while Beta 02 values remain historical unless the live build or a current note reconfirms them.

The September 12 Patch 0.11 notice reports **400,000 peak concurrent players**. Third-party SteamDB tracking recorded a higher **428,666** peak on September 13; that number is a third-party measurement, not an official figure.

A separate September 10 notice reports **1.25 million copies sold**, and the official WARDOGS account announced **2 million copies sold** on September 15. Sales and concurrency must not be combined, and no page here presents a live player counter.

## What BULKHEAD says comes next

The studio describes Early Access as a one-to-two-year process and lists these as planned rather than delivered: more maps, weapons, and vehicles, additional objective variants, a deeper progression system, and a larger seasonal metagame. Fighter jets have also been discussed publicly. Console versions are announced for 2028, with PS5 and Xbox named at launch and exact dates still unconfirmed.

Shorter-term Patch 0.11 follow-ups named by the developer are Community server favourites, tiered priority queues, a Message of the Day prompt, improved warmup activities, AFK-kick changes during seeding, and team-switching controls. None of these are shipped until a later note says so.

## Beta history is not an Early Access patch

Closed Beta 02 ended on September 6. Its temporary access rules, prices, balance values, queues, workarounds, and observed bugs are historical test evidence. They must be reproduced in the Early Access build before being presented as current behavior.

The September 5 Open Beta notice changed test access, not launch balance. The earlier beta recap also describes an ended build. Neither should be renamed or indexed as September 10 patch notes.

## How updates will be recorded

Every entry will separate confirmed changes, unresolved wording, and community observations. If a note omits an exact value, this tracker will not invent one. If the live interface differs from a written note, both the build and observation date will be recorded until an official clarification arrives.

## Player checklist after a download

Record the Steam download time, visible build identifier, and any reset settings. Recheck the server region, voice input, graphics preset, keybinds, inventory prices, and progression state before applying an old workaround. Do not disable anti-cheat or install unsigned tools to recover access.
