## Update 0.1.2: September 30 minor patch

The [official September 30 Update 0.1.2 announcement](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1867240/view/712287592723252267) records a fix for the Windows 11 KB5124010 issue causing WD-L020 crashes, cash/XP exploit fixes, and changes to the Deploy server browser. Its announced 08:00 UTC maintenance window, estimated at one hour, is a dated deployment notice, not current service telemetry.

- **WD-L020:** the fix targets the KB5124010-related Windows 11 branch; it does not guarantee that every startup error or crash is resolved.
- **Cash and XP:** revive validity checks are now server authoritative; vehicle fly-through rewards are capped; rewards for destroying buildables scale with build progress; depositing pallets into a FOB outside the zone gives reduced rewards.
- **Deploy:** choose Official, Community, Infantry Mode or Low-Level servers. Official advanced filters are restored. The browser shows connecting/queue state after Join, full names on hover, selected-region counts and gamepad support on the Deploy selection screen. Use the [Infantry Mode guide](/en/guides/wardogs-infantry-mode) to check what a specific server still allows before spending cash.
- **Other changes:** community-server restart scheduling and RCON/security controls, server stability/security hotfixes, enclosed-seat throwable restrictions, crosshair and buildable crit-spot fixes, a Havoc 20mm/30mm damage fix and localisation improvements.

### October 2: two hotfix records to distinguish

The [official feed](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1867240/announcements/) announced security/stability maintenance for **October 2 at 08:00 UTC**, with an expected one-hour outage. That is a dated planned window; it is not proof that servers remain offline or that every launch code was fixed.

A separate October 2 **IR Goggles & CWIS Balance Hotfix** required no new client download or server downtime. It disabled new IR Rangefinder purchases at the vendor as regions restarted, until Season 02, and changed the announced CWIS-versus-Havoc time from about 12 seconds to about 6 seconds. Read the [equipment changes](/en/guides/wardogs-equipment-tools-guide) for purchase and balance scope, and [server status](/en/guides/wardogs-server-status) for a connection failure. The [Season 02 date and reset rules](/en/guides/wardogs-progression-wipes-guide) are a separate transition.

## Patch 0.11: published September 14 maintenance window

The dated Patch 0.11 announcement is retained as a source checkpoint. Its published September 14 08:00 UTC maintenance window has passed. The announcement covered separate Official and Community server browsers, search and filter improvements, persistent server IDs, cash and GPU fixes, and additional Asia capacity. Check the official Steam feed for newer incidents; this site does not operate live telemetry.

### Confirmed for Patch 0.11

- Separate **Community** and **Official** server browsers.
- More reliable Community filters, text search, persistent server IDs, and Show Empty/Full controls.
- A corrected Host link plus normalized server ordering and naming.
- Fixes for multiple cash exploits and more GPU crash paths.
- Increased server capacity in Asia.
- A 5% End of Match cash bonus on Community servers for the following week.

### Announced for later, not included yet

Community favourites, tiered priority queues, a Message of the Day prompt, improved warmup activities, seeding-specific AFK controls, and team-switching controls are future work. They are not Patch 0.11 features until a later note says they shipped.

Finish a match before maintenance, let Steam update the main WARDOGS app, and check the official notice before retrying. The developer says repeated cash abuse, exploit tutorials, and XP farming can lead to resets or bans.

## What counts as an official patch note

A tracked release needs a first-party Steam announcement or a directly attributable BULKHEAD, Team17, or WARDOGS notice. Each entry should include the publication date, build or version identifier when supplied, affected systems, and a source link.

Server maintenance and emergency notices are recorded separately from client patches. A server restart may change availability without downloading a new build; a Steam download may also contain packaging changes that are not a documented balance patch.

## Current launch watchlist

- Steam release state and the first installable Early Access branch.
- Login queue, entitlement, server browser, region, and voice-service notices.
- Crash, anti-cheat, RAM, VRAM, upscaling, shadow, and Reflex changes.
- Weapon, armor, ammunition, vehicle, cash, progression, and unlock adjustments.
- Controller, HOTAS, keybind, UI, accessibility, and localization corrections.

These are monitoring categories, not claims that a change has occurred.

## Confirmed Facts

WARDOGS launched in Early Access on September 10. The official Season 1 changelog and Launch Stability Hotfix #1 are now available, while Beta 02 values remain historical unless the live build or a current note reconfirms them.

The September 12 Patch 0.11 notice also reports **400,000 peak concurrent players**. That is an official launch milestone, not a live player counter. A separate September 10 notice reports **1.25 million copies sold**; sales and concurrency must not be combined.

## Season 1 progression changes

The launch changelog replaced the short beta progression curve. Levels below 6 were unchanged, while the published cumulative XP bands changed as follows:

| Level | Beta XP | Season 1 XP | Change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 6 | 8,750 | 9,250 | 5.7% slower |
| 10 | 25,000 | 35,000 | 40.0% slower |
| 15 | 54,750 | 110,000 | 100.9% slower |
| 20 | 111,500 | 250,000 | 124.2% slower |
| 25 | 192,000 | 325,000 | 69.3% slower |
| 35 | 416,500 | 500,000 | 20.0% slower |

The official target described a median player reaching about level 20 after 20–25 hours. Artillery moved to Career level 90 and the Heavy Tank moved to Driver level 35. These are Season 1 launch values and can change in a later patch.

## How to Use This Guide

Compare the publication date and build identifier with your Steam client. Read server notices separately from client changes, then revisit any affected specialist guide for practical steps.

## Beta history is not an Early Access patch

Closed Beta 02 ended on September 6. Its temporary access rules, prices, balance values, queues, workarounds, and observed bugs are historical test evidence. They must be reproduced in the Early Access build before being presented as current behavior.

The September 5 Open Beta notice changed test access, not launch balance. The earlier beta recap also describes an ended build. Neither should be renamed or indexed as September 10 patch notes.

## How updates will be recorded

Every entry will separate confirmed changes, unresolved wording, and community observations. If a note omits an exact value, this tracker will not invent one. If the live interface differs from a written note, both the build and observation date will be recorded until an official clarification arrives.

## Player checklist after a download

Record the Steam download time, visible build identifier, and any reset settings. Recheck the server region, voice input, graphics preset, keybinds, inventory prices, and progression state before applying an old workaround. Do not disable anti-cheat or install unsigned tools to recover access.
