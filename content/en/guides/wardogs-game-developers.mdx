WARDOGS is developed by **BULKHEAD** and published by **Team17**. BULKHEAD creates the game and describes it as an in-development large-scale FPS; Team17 handles the publishing role shown on the official product pages.

## BULKHEAD

BULKHEAD's WARDOGS page describes tactical gunplay, vehicles, building, destruction, a large sandbox, and persistent cash. The studio is the primary source for development intent, build changes, technical priorities, testing context, and explanations of how systems are designed.

Developer credit does not support guesses about the exact team size, individual responsibilities, budget, employment structure, ownership, or internal schedule. Unless BULKHEAD publishes those details, they should remain unclaimed.

## Team17

Team17 is the publisher listed for WARDOGS on Steam and its own product materials. A publisher can coordinate release, store presence, marketing, trailers, press resources, distribution, and commercial communication. The exact division of every task between publisher and studio is not public, so broad industry assumptions should not be turned into WARDOGS-specific facts.

Team17's official pages and channel are strong sources for platform announcements, trailers, press assets, and release communication. Steam remains the final store record for account-specific pricing, requirements, access controls, and purchase state.

## Roles

The clean credit line is simple: **Developer: BULKHEAD. Publisher: Team17.** Do not reverse the labels, collapse both organizations into a single studio, or imply that a community wiki, subreddit, creator channel, FirstLook page, or Discord moderator is part of the development team.

WARDOGS itself is the game and brand. Official social and community accounts can relay news without changing the legal or production credits displayed on the product pages.

## Official Channels
