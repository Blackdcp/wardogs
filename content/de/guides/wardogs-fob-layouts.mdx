## Schnelle Antwort

**Start with three templates: a beginner tower FOB, an anti-mortar dispersed FOB, and a logistics staging FOB. Keep spawn, unload, repair, ammo and observation paths separated enough that one mortar correction does not erase the base.**

Diese Seite ist als aktuelle Suchseite aufgebaut: Sie gibt eine direkte Antwort, verlinkt das passende Werkzeug und markiert unsichere Werte, bis eine Primärquelle sie bestätigt.

## Bestätigte Fakten

- FOBs connect building, spawning, supply, fire support and defense decisions.
- Community logistics content is strong because truck routes and build order decide whether the FOB matters.
- Update 0.1.2 reward changes make off-zone pallet abuse weaker, increasing the value of useful frontline supply.
- Exact structure caps and live costs remain build-sensitive and should be checked in client.

## Aktionsplan

### Beginner tower FOB

Place close enough to reinforce a tower but far enough that fresh spawns do not appear in the obvious kill lane.

### Anti-mortar FOB

Separate the spawn, ammo, repair and mortar pieces. If the first shell finds one asset, the second should not delete the whole layout.

### Logistics staging FOB

Protect the Ural approach, leave a clear unload lane and mark a fallback route before contact.

### Fire-support FOB

Only add mortars when spotting, supply and relocation are planned. An isolated tube attracts counterfire.

### Audit after each fight

After every successful defense, ask what the enemy learned about the layout and move the obvious second target.
