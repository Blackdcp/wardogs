## Resposta rápida

**Use the roadmap as a confidence-ranked tracker. Season 2 date and storm teaser are confirmed; exact patch notes, balance values, new equipment lists, server-tool details and console timing need their own dated source before becoming promises.**

## Fatos confirmados

- Season 02 is dated October 15, 2026.
- The teaser visually signals storm or wet battlefield conditions but does not publish a full weather system spec.
- Update 0.1.2 says a larger Season 02 update will include new map conditions, equipment, balance reworks and additional features.
- Console plans exist in official material, but PC Early Access is the current product focus.

## Plano de ação

### Separate confirmed from expected

A date, teaser shot and patch note sentence do not carry the same confidence.

### Track feature families

Weather, equipment, balance, server tools and metagame systems deserve separate rows.

### Update after every announcement

Roadmap pages win only if they retire old speculation quickly.

### Link to decisions

Every roadmap item should send users to a practical guide: wipe, loadouts, servers, FOBs or tools.

### Avoid overpromising

If an exact weapon, vehicle or map is not named in an official source, label it pending.
