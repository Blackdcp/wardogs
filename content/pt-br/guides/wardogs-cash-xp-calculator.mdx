## Resposta rápida

**Use the calculator as a planning layer, not an official payout table. Enter a reward you actually observed in the current client, test zone or repeat assumptions, and compare the result with your reserve and Season 2 plan.**

## Plano de ação

### Record the base reward

Write down the cash and XP from one completed action before stacking bonuses or assumptions.

### Separate cash from XP

A task can be useful for cash, role XP, Career XP or battlefield impact. Do not merge them into one score.

### Apply context deliberately

Population, objective state, Hot Zone and repeat penalties should be toggled consciously, not hidden in a headline.

### Compare with replacement cost

A profitable action can still be bad if it burns a vehicle, kit or time window.

### Recheck after patches

Patch notes and server-side changes can invalidate yesterday’s route.
