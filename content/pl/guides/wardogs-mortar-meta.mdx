## Szybka odpowiedź

**The counter to mortar pressure is not one trick. Use map measurement, spread FOB assets, hide the resupply lane, force the firing team to relocate, and reserve SPH-2 or counter-battery only when spotting can confirm the target.**

## Plan działania

### Measure before firing

Use the map and artillery calculator for a first estimate; confirm with a spotting correction before a volley.

### Do not stack FOB assets

Mortar pressure punishes compact bases. Separate spawn, ammo and vehicle repair functions.

### Hunt the firing loop

Look for repeated origin direction, resupply timing and correction cadence instead of chasing every impact crater.

### Use SPH-2 deliberately

A self-propelled gun is a team investment. It needs crew, protection, ammo and a relocation route.

### Log balance changes

If mortar camera or splash behavior changes, update tactics before preserving old meta advice.
