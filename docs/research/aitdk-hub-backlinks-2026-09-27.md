## Execution and measurement gates

- No new third-party submission was sent as part of this screenshot audit. The local Chrome AITDK screenshots are sufficient for triage; they are not a session that this task can operate. Browser-control attempts ended at the safety boundary while determining the Windows browser URL, and the in-app browser bridge could not load its request-header policy. Do not bypass those controls or claim a listing exists.
- Before sending any editorial request: identify the exact live article, its current claim, the first-party correction, and a legitimate contact channel. One personalized request per publisher; no paid links, duplicate community posts or followed-anchor demands.
- Count a win only after the public referring page and destination both load. Track referral sessions, not just link counts, and compare query/page search performance in the owner's GSC. AITDK's modeled DR, traffic and backlink totals are directional competitor signals, not our KPI.
