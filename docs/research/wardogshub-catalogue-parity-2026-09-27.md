1. Correct current-version conflicts first: Z20 vs UH-1Y; keep old evidence in history. Remove the unwanted visible owner-art caption while retaining internal provenance. Keep the four identifier-only weapon records and three variant-only vehicle records from being mistaken for confirmed current inventory.
2. Bring catalogue discoverability to 15/15 with real destinations, not blank shells: reuse the existing six strong sections; add a helicopter view and dedicated evidence-backed sections for tier, economy, markets, skins, TTK and artillery only as their data/logic is ready. Each route needs unique useful content, navigation, metadata, and cross-links.
3. Fill item-by-item gaps in equipment, gear and attachments; capture or license record-specific game images. Competitor images are comparison evidence only. Add data-driven tests for coverage, missing imagery, stale build labels and bad route links.
4. Release verified batches after unit/content/type/build/browser checks and inspect production routes. Do not label 15/15 complete until every destination actually works and catalogue rows have source-appropriate evidence.
