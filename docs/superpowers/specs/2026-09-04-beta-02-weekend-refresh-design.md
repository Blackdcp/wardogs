# WARDOGS Closed Beta 02 Weekend Refresh Design

## Goal

Bring every public surface in line with the live September 3-6 Closed Beta 02 window, publish the newly announced $100K clip contest and a safe known-issues hub, strengthen current-player discovery, and ship the six-language update without weakening the site's evidence standards.

## Current Event Contract

- Closed Beta 02 runs from September 3, 2026 at 18:00 UTC through September 6, 2026 at 08:00 UTC.
- A paid Steam pre-purchase guarantees access; free Steam Playtest access is granted in waves.
- The event is a closed scale test, not an open beta.
- Steam Early Access remains scheduled for September 10, 2026.
- The September 3 FPS Games Show is complete. Its WARDOGS announcement was the $100K clip contest, so no page may keep describing the reveal as unknown or upcoming.
- The August Closed Beta and September 2 Firing Range test remain available only as clearly labeled history.

## Content Scope

- Update the home page, news timeline, and the access, launch, download, livestream, settings, crash, controls, PS5, factions, and Twitch Drops journeys.
- Add independent six-language guides for the $100K clip contest and Closed Beta 02 known issues.
- Keep Twitch reward names and thresholds unconfirmed until a first-party campaign page supplies them.
- Treat performance workarounds and creator observations as hardware- and build-sensitive.

## Visual And Catalogue Scope

- Keep existing source-linked weapon, vehicle, ammunition, attachment, gear, and faction imagery visible.
- Add a current-build disclosure above the catalogue so Alpha and August Closed Beta values cannot be mistaken for Closed Beta 02 balance.
- Fix meaningful home imagery alternative text and preserve decorative treatment for the wordmark.

## Technical Scope

- Remove obsolete sponsored-copy strings and verify that no Adsterra or arkgleamfox code remains.
- Preserve AdSense, consent, analytics, sitemap, and IndexNow behavior.
- Strengthen metadata on the Bing-flagged short pages and investigate the GA `(not set)` symptom without inventing a client-side page view.
- Verify content, unit tests, lint, typecheck, production build, responsive rendering, public routes, sitemap, ads.txt, and deployment.

## Source Rules

Official WARDOGS, Steam, Team17, BULKHEAD, the contest microsite, and developer-authored Steam support posts lead. Press may corroborate dates. Community reports may identify symptoms but never become universal fixes, final balance values, or official outage claims.
