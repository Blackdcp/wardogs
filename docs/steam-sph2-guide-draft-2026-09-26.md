## The short answer

WARDOGS has two very different artillery costs. BULKHEAD's Season 1 changelog moved the Artillery Tank category to Career level 90 and set its **one-time unlock at $500,000**. That is not what you pay each time you spawn a vehicle. The official note does not publish a current SPH-2 repeat vendor price. Two separate Season 1 players report paying **$8,000 for an artillery deployment**, but I have not verified a current-client vendor screenshot, so treat the number as a player report that may change after a patch.

## What each source actually establishes

1. **Official launch changelog:** Career 90 and $500,000 for the Artillery Tank category. The note does not identify the SPH-2 by model name or list a current deployment price. Source: https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1867240/view/701027323413004455
2. **Earlier Alpha/Beta footage:** The older vendor observations do not define today's price. An Alpha capture showed $10,000; later Closed Beta creator footage showed $8,000 with a different one-time unlock. That old unlock was superseded by the Season 1 official note. Footage: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oP9RelmWk6A
3. **Season 1 player reports:** One SPH-2 discussion reports $8,000 to deploy and roughly $11,000–$13,000 with ammunition: https://www.reddit.com/r/OfficialWARDOGS/comments/1wj2f29/sph_2/ . A separate owner reports an $8,000 deployment, $60 per artillery shell, and about $9,400 for their loaded sortie: https://www.reddit.com/r/WarDogs/comments/1wjbvaa/what_are_your_thoughts_on_how_cheap_this_is/ . These are not official price-sheet entries and the loadout totals differ because the purchases differ.

## How to budget a sortie

Write down **vehicle + shells + crew/personal gear + any support spending**. If the $8,000 vehicle and $60 shell reports still match your vendor, then 20 shells would make $9,200 before the other purchases; 40 shells would make $10,400 before them. These are conditional arithmetic examples, not a verified shop quote or a recommendation to load exactly 20 or 40 shells.

For one-match cash break-even, compare the actual payout from your sortie with everything that sortie cost. Do **not** add the $500,000 permanent unlock to each trip. If you want to decide whether the unlock was worth buying across many matches, track your profit after each trip and compare the cumulative total with the one-time fee. Both questions are legitimate, but they answer different things.

## Do not overlook the tactical cost

Old creator footage shows why a cheap hull does not guarantee profit: the driver has to find stable ground and an escape route, the gunner must range and correct shots, and a third player can watch for drones, helicopters or infantry. A crew sitting still outside the Control Zone may give up other objective income and become easy to hunt. These are operating lessons from footage, not promises about the current build's range, armor or controls. Check the live vehicle UI and vendor before spending.
